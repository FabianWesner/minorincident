# Retained authored LOD budgets

Art registration wave 0 retains existing authored distance geometry for the following assets. A percentage alone cannot account for independent closed panels, limbs, hidden stump caps and assemblies with multiple bodies. Absolute budgets are rounded to the next 1,000 triangles from the retained exports, and remain enforced per asset. House and vehicle caps are unchanged. These exceptions are a production scope decision, not proof of frame budgets: dense later-level scenery still needs native distance authoring/performance QA.

Native food-court, mini-mall and hospital tiers were rebuilt separately. The mini-mall has a small authored-cap exception for its retained closed surfaces.
