# PO campaign update — Levels 4 to 9 (2026-10-11, verbatim)

> Source of truth for L4–L9. Wins over the epic specs and over `po-levels-2-6-2026-10-07.md` on any disagreement. Orchestrator decisions on how to implement it are in the "Orchestrator decisions" section at the end.

Here is the updated campaign from Level 4 onward, including the changes and the new levels we discussed.

Level 4: The Safe City Falls

The army convoy brings the player to Fairhaven, which looks untouched. Clean streets, open shops, civilians, soldiers, defensive positions. It should feel like a genuine rescue and a false reset after the collapse of the first city.

The player gets a machine gun. There is no ammo system, so it can be used continuously. Soldiers are heavily armed and initially look capable of controlling the situation.

The outbreak starts inside the perimeter, among the evacuees. People turn behind the military lines, civilians panic and block fields of fire, and new infected appear faster than soldiers can contain them.

Level 4 also introduces the first Alpha infected. They are rare, very large, extremely strong human infected inspired by the Alpha idea from 28 Years Later. They are not fantasy monsters or bosses, but unusually resilient infected that can absorb concentrated gunfire and physically break defensive lines. Their real tactical value is that soldiers have to focus on them while regular infected overwhelm the gaps.

The battle escalates into full-scale urban warfare with soldiers, armed civilians, military vehicles and huge numbers of infected. Everyone is fighting, but it still is not enough.

The player eventually escapes alone into the basement of a hardware store and hides there while the city collapses above.

---

Level 5: Aftermath

Several hours later, around sunset, the player emerges from the basement into the same Fairhaven.

The battle is over.

The same streets from Level 4 are now devastated and almost silent: burned vehicles, corpses, broken windows, damaged buildings, fires, smoke, wrecked military equipment and scattered debris. The recognizable environment should make the destruction more impactful.

At the start of the level, the player can find a shotgun in the hardware-store basement.

The player still has access to previous weapons, including the machine gun.

The shotgun has a distinct role:

- very strong single-target damage
- excellent at close range
- normal infected can be killed quickly
- especially useful against Alphas
- machine gun remains better against large groups

The mission is to find five survivors hidden throughout Fairhaven.

There are only scattered infected now, not huge hordes. The challenge is protecting the growing survivor group. A single infected that reaches the civilians can still create a serious problem.

The player finds the survivors one by one and escorts whoever survives to a metro entrance.

Inside the station, around 20 to 30 additional survivors have already gathered.

The level ends with the group entering the metro and the doors closing behind them.

---

Level 6: The Subway

Level 6 starts inside the metro shelter.

The survivors from Level 5 are there, together with roughly 20 to 30 other people. For a short time, the atmosphere is calm. People rest, talk, treat wounds and share supplies.

There is no new weapon. The player keeps the existing arsenal. The level is about protecting people, not another weapon upgrade.

Infected begin entering through maintenance tunnels and underground access routes.

Initially it is only one or two. Then more appear.

The main power fails and the station switches to red emergency lighting.

The shelter has to be abandoned.

The player leads the survivor group down the track tunnel, protecting them through narrow spaces and chokepoints. The danger comes from keeping a large vulnerable group alive in confined darkness.

Eventually the group reaches another exit and climbs back above ground.

It is now night.

---

Level 7: The Ruined City

The player exits the subway into the same city, but while everyone was underground the situation became dramatically worse.

The city is now completely overrun:

- huge zombie populations
- major fires
- collapsed and heavily damaged buildings
- wrecked military vehicles
- destroyed streets
- almost no functioning defensive positions

This should be the most destroyed version of the city so far.

Immediately near the subway exit is a wrecked military vehicle or position where the player can obtain a rocket launcher.

The rocket launcher has unlimited ammunition.

This changes the tone of the game significantly.

Level 7 is deliberately a power-fantasy combat level after the quieter, tense Levels 5 and 6.

The player now has access to the whole accumulated arsenal:

- bat
- axe
- handgun
- machine gun
- shotgun
- rocket launcher

The streets contain enormous groups of regular infected and multiple Alphas.

The rocket launcher allows the player to destroy large groups at once. The player should be able to fire into a dense crowd and clear a substantial section immediately.

There are no infected zoo animals in Level 7.

The objective is not simply to kill endlessly. The survivor group is still trying to escape the city.

The player must blast a route toward the final evacuation corridor at the edge of the city.

The final section contains a huge horde blocking the exit. The player uses the rocket launcher and other weapons to open a path while survivors move through behind them.

At the far end is a working bus or transport vehicle.

The survivors board it and finally leave the city.

The last view is the burning skyline disappearing behind them as they drive into the night.

---

Level 8: The Zoo

The evacuation vehicle reaches a large zoo.

The zoo has been converted into a safe zone.

It initially looks genuinely secure:

- strong perimeter walls
- military presence
- civilians
- normal zoo staff or survivors
- intact facilities
- normal animals
- clean paths
- lights
- functioning infrastructure

There may already be infected visible outside the walls, but they cannot enter.

The player can freely walk around the zoo.

There is deliberately no immediate objective.

For roughly the first 60 seconds, nothing major happens.

This should create false safety.

Then infected suddenly begin entering from below.

The zoo has an underground tunnel or maintenance network that the military failed to secure. Zombies emerge from several locations inside the zoo simultaneously.

They attack:

- civilians
- soldiers
- zoo staff
- animals

The animals can then become infected as well.

This introduces the infected zoo animals that were already prepared for the game, including creatures such as infected lions and other zoo animals.

The zoo turns from a safe sanctuary into another massive outbreak.

The player keeps all existing weapons. There is no need for another new weapon.

Level 8 becomes another large-scale combat level, but it feels different from Level 7 because the threat now includes:

- regular infected
- Alphas
- infected civilians
- infected zoo animals

The player fights through the zoo toward an escape point.

The goal is not to clear the zoo. It is to reach an armored military vehicle.

The level ends when the player and surviving group escape in that vehicle.

---

Level 9: The Crossing

Level 9 starts immediately inside the armored military vehicle from Level 8.

This is a large, open level between the zoo and the next city.

The environment should feel much more spacious than the previous urban maps:

- open roads
- countryside
- dry terrain or desert-like areas
- long sight lines
- many different possible routes

The destination is visible conceptually: reach the next city.

The vehicle has no mounted guns.

Its only offensive ability is its mass.

The player can run zombies over.

However, every collision with an infected damages the vehicle.

The vehicle has its own damage bar, and that damage cannot be repaired during the level.

Zombies can also attack the vehicle directly if the player becomes surrounded or stops.

This creates the central decision:

Drive through the horde and lose durability, or steer around them and preserve the vehicle.

The vehicle is powerful but temporary.

The player should be able to:

- use roads
- leave the road
- drive across open terrain
- avoid large zombie groups
- intentionally smash through smaller groups
- choose riskier shortcuts

If the vehicle reaches zero durability, it breaks down.

The player then has to leave it and continue the remaining journey on foot with their normal weapons.

That means two players can experience the level differently:

- one preserves the vehicle almost to the end
- another destroys it early and has a much longer survival walk

The level ends when the player reaches the next city or its outskirts.

---

Updated escalation

The second half of the campaign now has a clearer rhythm:

L4: Army power fails against internal outbreak + first Alphas
L5: Quiet aftermath + shotgun + survivor rescue
L6: Claustrophobic metro escape
L7: Full power fantasy with unlimited rocket launcher
L8: False sanctuary at zoo → infected animals + mass battle
L9: Open-world-style armored vehicle crossing with limited vehicle durability
L10: Still open, to be designed later

The important weapon progression is now:

Bat → Axe → Handgun → Machine gun → Shotgun → no new weapon → Rocket launcher → no new weapon → armored vehicle as temporary gameplay power

And the city/world progression becomes:

intact → wounded → collapsing → war zone → silent ruins → underground → completely destroyed → zoo sanctuary fails → open-country escape.

## Orchestrator decisions (2026-10-11, changeable)

- **Alpha** is a new human infected archetype (`inf.alpha`): derived from the existing infected human base model, about 1.35–1.5× scale, heavy build, damage resistance so concentrated fire is needed (shotgun clearly best single-target, machine gun needs a sustained burst), knocks soldiers down and breaks barricades/sandbag lines (E26 props). PO 2026-10-11 verbatim: "Alpha should have a health bar and special moves". **Health bar:** world-space bar above the Alpha, shown once it is damaged or within ~15 m of the player, readable on phone. **Special moves (orchestrator default, each telegraphed ≥ 0.6 s, with cooldowns):** *Charge* — straight sprint that knocks down soldiers/civilians/player in its path and smashes barricades; *Ground slam* — radial knockdown around it; *Grab and throw* — picks up a body or medium prop (E26) and throws it; *Roar* — nearby regular infected speed up briefly and converge. Still not a fantasy monster. Appears: L4 2–3, L5 1–2, L7 several, L8 several.
- **Shotgun** and **rocket launcher** reuse the existing E06 catalog weapons. Shotgun pickup in the Kessler's Hardware basement at L5 start; rocket launcher at a wrecked army vehicle beside the L6 exit at L7 start; rocket launcher unlimited, with blast radius large enough to clear a dense crowd section (E27 explosions, performance budget held).
- **L5 end** changes: the escort reaches the metro entrance, 20–30 survivors are already inside, doors close (cut to L6). L6 stays as specified.
- **L7** reuses the Fairhaven district (`D-FAIR`) with a new, heaviest decay tier at night (W6), from the L6 exit to an evacuation corridor at the city edge; final horde; bus departure cutscene with the burning skyline.
- **L8** new district `D-ZOO`: perimeter walls, enclosures with normal animals, 60 s calm with no objective, then tunnel emergence at ≥ 3 points inside; animals can be infected (existing infected animal archetypes, including lion); exit at an armored military vehicle.
- **Infected zoo animals** (PO 2026-10-11 verbatim, after the Alpha line: "Same for Zoo animals"): every infected zoo animal has the same world-space health bar as the Alpha and telegraphed special moves (≥ 0.6 s, cooldowns). Existing assets: `inf.lion`, `inf.gorilla`, `inf.flamingo`; normal zoo animals `amb.elephant`, `amb.zebra` need infected variants derived from those models. Moves (orchestrator default): *lion* — pounce that pins a target, flanking sprint; *gorilla* — chest-beat roar (nearby infected converge), ground pound knockdown, grab and throw props/bodies; *elephant* — stampede charge that knocks over people, barricades and light vehicles, trunk swipe; *zebra* — rear kick knockback, herd stampede; *flamingo* — flock dive that briefly blocks the view and staggers. Normal (uninfected) animals have no health bar.
- **L9** new open terrain level (`D-CROSSING`): roads, countryside, dry terrain, several routes; armored vehicle with no weapon, durability bar (no repair), damage per infected collision and from infected attacking it when stopped or surrounded; at 0 it breaks down and the player continues on foot; ends at the next city's outskirts. Performance: terrain streaming and LOD so the open level holds the E18 budgets.
- **L10**: the grand finale (PO 2026-10-11: "Level 10 will be the grande finale then, I will provide story line tomorrow"). Not specified until the PO storyline arrives.
- Order: specs → L4 Alphas + L5 shotgun/ending → L7 → L8 → L9.

- **L4 collapse design (PO 2026-10-12 approved: "ok let's try that!")**: outbreak starts behind the lines (15–25 evacuees turn at once); bitten soldiers/civilians turn within seconds (snowball, infected soldiers in uniform); a collapse director forces beats — ~60 s line 1 overrun, ~2 min Alphas break gun nests and a barricade, ~3 min APC/tank swarmed, then "Fall back!" and the player escapes alone to the basement. If a line holds too well the director adds infected inside/flank; soldier accuracy is never nerfed. Outside infected ×3–5 with replenishment within E18 budgets. Brief: epics-pipeline/briefs/l4-collapse.md.

- **L3 balance (PO 2026-10-12, verbatim, supersedes "keep police strength"):** "L3. I am just watching the figt police vs Zombies. Ideally the Zombies will win against police when the player isn#t doing anything. Let's try this: reduce the power of the gun by 50% for the police (not for the player). And don't cluster Zombies, let they come from all directions and also spawn them in the policestation (as police Zombies), so they are overwhelming for the police so the user needs to start running away (with some of the survivors)"

- **Infected soldiers (PO 2026-10-13, verbatim):** "When a solidier becomes a Zombie, then this Zombie has 50% more health" → turned soldiers get 1.5× the HP of the infected archetype they become (all levels; police zombies: see next line).
- **Infected police (PO 2026-10-13, verbatim):** "When a police officer becomes a Zombie, then this Zombie has 25% more health" → turned police officers (incl. the L3 station police zombies) get 1.25× the HP of the infected archetype they become.
