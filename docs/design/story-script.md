# Minor Incident — endgame story script (L8 → L9 → L10)

Author: the orchestrator, at the PO's request ("For story line, I prefer YOU do it yourself", 2026-11-08).
This script is the single source for story beats, captions and dialogue in L8–L10. It supersedes the story parts of
`endgame-story.md` and `l9-l10-concept.md` where they differ; their gameplay sections still apply.
Lanes copy lines from here into `src/data/dialogue.ts`; they do not invent story text.

## Premise (what the player already knows from L1–L7)

The courier delivered one cold-chain parcel to the Medical Annex in L1. Minutes later the outbreak began.
Since then she has run with whoever was left — firefighters, police, the army — found Sam, Mia, Rosa, Dale and
Mr. Okafor in the ruins, got them through the subway and out of the dead city on an evacuation bus.
The corgi has been with her all day. She has never said out loud what she suspects: that the parcel started it.

## The arc in one line

She started it with a delivery. She ends it with a delivery.
L8 is the last safe place. L9 is the last time she has everything. L10 is alone, hunted, and the way back.

## L8 — The Zoo ("The last safe place")

**Beat 1 · Sanctuary (calm, ~1 min).** The bus rolls into the zoo, now an army sanctuary. A keeper, **Nadia**, leads
the group along lit enclosures: flamingos, zebras, the lion, the gorilla, the old elephant. Soldiers, staff, children
pointing at animals. It is the first time all day nobody is running.
- Objective: *Follow Nadia to the central plaza.*
- Nadia: "Stay on the path. They're nervous tonight — so are we."
- Nadia (at the lion): "Sixteen years old. Grumpiest cat in the state."
- Radio at the command post (caption): "…research campus past Hollow Creek reports a counter-agent… evacuation from the Hollow Creek bridge at dawn… all units…"
- Major **Hale** (to the courier): "You're the courier. Heard about you. Get some rest — you've earned one night."

**Beat 2 · From below.** The service tunnels under the zoo were never sealed. Infected pour out of three maintenance
hatches, army dogs among them — turned K9s that still wear their vests.
- Objective: *Protect the group!*
- Hale: "Tunnels! Get the civilians to the plaza!"
- The player watches a keeper get pulled down at the flamingo pond, and a flamingo turn.

**Beat 3 · Zoo outbreak (the animal fights).** The animals she just admired turn, one enclosure after another.
Each is a fight with its own rules (see `endgame-story.md`, arena table): flamingo swarm, zebra stampede, the monkey
pack, the lion that stalks, the gorilla that throws, the elephant that charges. Rockets are scarce here.
- Objective: *Lead the group to the armored vehicle.*
- Nadia (if alive): "Not him. Not the old one…" (the elephant)
- Courier (first and only spoken line in L8, after the elephant falls): "I'm sorry."

**Beat 4 · Hold at the APC.** The group boards; she covers them, boarding last.
- Objective: *Cover the survivors while they board · n/N aboard.*
- Then something comes over the north wall. Bigger than any Alpha. Soldiers empty their magazines into it; it does not slow.
  It reaches Hale at the APC door.
- Hale (dying, pressing the keys into her hand): "Hollow Creek. Get them out."

**Beat 5 · Departure.** She drives out through the east gate. In the mirror the thing is running after them.
- Caption: *Something is following you.*

## L9 — The Crossing ("The last time she has everything")

She drives the APC across open country at night with the survivors aboard, racing the migrating horde to the bridge.
This is the level where she has every weapon and every friend — and the level that takes them.

- Opening (Sam, from the back): "Bridge is forty klicks. Dawn's in two hours. Floor it."
- Clingers on the hull — Mia: "They're on the roof!" — Courier: "Hold on to something."
- The school bus full of infected behind them — Dale: "That's my kid's school bus." (beat) "It's not anymore."
- Stall on the rail tracks: she climbs into the hatch and fights while Sam restarts the engine. — Sam: "Thirty seconds! Give me thirty seconds!"
- The closing evacuation gate — Radio: "Gate closes in sixty. Anyone not through stays." 
- Bridgehead: the army trucks take Mia, Rosa, Dale and Mr. Okafor north. Rosa hugs the corgi.
  - Mr. Okafor: "You came back for us. Every time."
  - Sam stays: "Someone has to watch your back. And somebody has to see what's on that campus."
- On the bridge the thing drops onto the hull. The span gives way. Slow motion: the APC tips, the weapons rack burns,
  the rocket launcher slides into the river.
- Crawl out of the wreck. Axe on her back. The corgi pulls at her sleeve.
- Caption (end of L9): *Everything went into the river. Almost everything.*

## L10 — The Cure ("Alone, hunted, and the way back")

She wakes on the wrong bank, alone with the corgi and the axe. Sam's voice crackles on the radio — he made it to the
other side of the creek and is heading for the research site. The thing climbs out of the wreck behind her.
From here on, it hunts her.

**Chapter 1 · The corn (farmland).** Dry, broken corn under a dead sky. Burned-out farmhouses and barns.
Things move in the rows.
- Objective: *Get away from the river.* → *Find a way through the farms.*
- Sam (radio): "You alive? …Good. Don't stop moving. That thing doesn't sleep."
- First sighting of the Mega within the first minutes, crossing a field she has just left.
- Sam (radio, when she hides in a culvert/barn gap): "It can't fit where you fit. Use that."
- Found: hunting rifle (farmhouse), machete (barn), nail gun (workshop), Molotovs (shed).
- Painted on a barn roof: HELP — 3 INSIDE. Inside: three graves dug by the last one.

**Chapter 2 · The restricted zone ("Area 51").** Fences, warning signs, dead searchlights, abandoned checkpoints,
a quarantine that went wrong. Stronger infected; packs of turned dogs.
- Objective: *Get through the restricted zone.*
- Sign: AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY — BIOHAZARD LEVEL 4.
- Found: assault rifle with a flashlight (guard tower), flashbangs (checkpoint locker).
- Sam (radio, breaking up): "…the hangar… they're still there… lights on…" (static)

**Chapter 3 · The hangar (the safe space).** A sealed research hangar, still holding. Generators hum, emergency light,
quarantine tents. No infected inside. A handful of army researchers and two guards. Sam is here, hurt but alive.
The music drops to a quiet theme. This is the breath before the end.
- Objective: *Enter the hangar.*
- Dr. **Elena Voss** (lead researcher): "You're the courier. The one from the Annex." (She knows.)
- Voss: "The sample you carried that morning came from here. We sent it to the Annex for testing. The container failed. That's on us. Not on you."
- Courier (her second line in the whole game): "I carried it."
- Voss: "Then carry this one." (She hands over the cure canister.) "It's airborne. It only kills the infected. The dispersal complex is two kilometres east. Plug it into the main console, hold it while it charges, and release it."
- Sergeant **Ruiz** (armory): "Your weapons came off the bridge wreck this morning. Cleaned them. Took the liberty." — she gets every weapon back.
- Voss (giving the flamethrower): "Prototype. Burns hot. Don't use it in the corn."
- Sam: "I'm coming." — Voss: "You're bleeding into my floor. You're staying." — Sam (to the courier): "Go finish your delivery."

**Chapter 4 · The delivery.** Out of the hangar toward the dispersal complex. The thing is waiting outside.
She runs, fights, uses every gap. At the complex she installs the canister and holds the console while it charges.
- Objective: *Bring the cure to the dispersal complex.* → *Install the cure.* → *Hold the console.*
- The final fight: the thing comes for the console. The courier has her whole arsenal and the flamethrower;
  the complex gives her machinery, fuel tanks and an electrified gate to use against it.
- When it falls — Voss (radio): "Release it. Now."
- Objective: *Release the cure.*

**Epilogue.** The mist rolls out over the corn, the farms, the river. Infected drop where they stand. Survivors step out
of hiding. The corgi barks at the sunrise. Her phone buzzes, the same sound as the first minute of the game:
**"Delivered ✓"**. Pull out. Title: **Minor Incident**.

## Continuity rules

- Named survivors only appear if alive in the campaign save. If Sam died earlier, **Private Reyes** (a zoo soldier,
  introduced in L8 beat 1 standing next to Hale) takes his lines in L9 and L10.
- Nadia can die in beat 3; her elephant line plays only if she is alive.
- The corgi is always present and is never harmed.
- The courier speaks exactly twice in the endgame: "I'm sorry." (L8) and "I carried it." (L10). Everything else is shown.
- The Mega is never named in dialogue; characters call it "that thing" or "it". The HUD may call it "Mega Alpha".
