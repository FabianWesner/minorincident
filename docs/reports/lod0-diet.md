# D-GROVE LOD0 diet

Partial result: 19 heavy assets rebuilt; the other 38 placement asset IDs already met the requested LOD0 thresholds. Remaining above 150k unique vertices or 400 KiB: bld.mainstreet-brick, bld.clinic-annex, bld.house-b, veh.pickup-white, veh.sedan-green, bld.cafe-corner, bld.gas-station, bld.house-c, bld.house-d, bld.house-e, bld.garage-detached, bld.fire-station, veh.pickup-red. Sizes are rounded KiB. Vertex counts count each shared POSITION stream once, matching uploaded buffers; summing every palette primitive overcounts shared geometry.

Builds use smaller bevel/radial segment counts and remove covered faces within the same visibility/motion assembly. Covered faces remain present for AO baking and are removed only at export, retaining baked AO and custom normals. Builds opt into normalized 14-bit position quantization. The packer welds normals only when the existing octahedral encoder ships exactly identical values; hard seams remain. No LOD0 collapse decimation is used. Lower tiers are authored against the reduced LOD0 delivery ratios and assembly hull; sockets, named transforms and removable groups remain separate. Static collision was regenerated by the project packer.

Ray pruning on inward-wound house-b window details, flattened sign lettering, stronger roof simplification, and changed house-c bevel weights were rejected after visible changes in camera comparisons. Full sign depth, house-b tile bevel segments, house-c edge weights, and D/E roof bevels were restored. House-b retains its original authoring geometry and uses only the packing optimization. The garage and depot retain their original bevel settings. Pickup-red retains its authored bevel segment counts, and the green sedan retains its broad body bevel segments and 14-bit positions. This is intentionally a partial load-time improvement: remaining file budgets need further geometry work with additional visual review.

Before/after comparison sheets use Eevee with backface culling at 900px, 25-degree vertical FOV, 36-degree elevation; close/zoom correspond to a 1.8m survivor at approximately 180/257 pixels. Sheets include placement angles, reverse view, and roof cutaway where applicable. Only one sheet per changed asset is retained in the scratch directory recorded in the JSON report.
