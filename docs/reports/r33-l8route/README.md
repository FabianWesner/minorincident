# r33-l8route: a linear route through the L8 zoo

PO 11-10: "The L8 needs a clear path the user has to move, not just free 'find your way' bc then the user can just walk away from all animals." Plan: docs/reports/r32-l8route-plan (approved, order flamingo, lion, monkey, zebra, gorilla, elephant).

## Built
- **Layout** (`layouts/D-ZOO/layout.py`, block added after all procedural dressing): 87 hedge modules (H1-H11), 12 jersey barriers where a line crosses a paved path (the army checkpoints; `prop.jersey-barrier` is now an allowed road exception in `validate.ts`), 63 fixed crowd-fence panels (primate yard x -28..14, z 8..32 with 5 m gates on x=-24; locked elephant paddock N and S gates; locked gorilla N gate), a 7.1 m gorilla east exit (3 fence panels removed) with a paved stub to the x=96 lane. All 306 older enclosure panels are now `fixed`. Hedge/fence ends stop 3 cm short of lane edges (the validator expands footprints by the lane half width). Deviation from the plan: the elephant paddock's south gate is sealed too (a turned elephant must not walk into the south field); the three dead-end gate road edges/nodes were dropped.
- **Layout diff proof** (`public/assets/layouts/D-ZOO.layout.json` vs main): 1657 -> 1778 placements; all 1616 surviving old placements identical in id, position and order except the `fixed` flag on 306 panels; 41 removed (24 trees, 8 flowers, 2 street trees, 3 flower beds, 1 small bush, 3 fence panels), 162 added with ids from `:1657`; colliders 523 -> 681. Other changed keys: `geometryHash` (also changes on an unchanged rebuild), `roads`, `surfaces`.
- **Sim** (`src/data/l8Bosses.ts`, `L8BossArenas.ts`, `LevelEightZoo.ts`): order and entry points; one gate rule `l8GateClosed` for sim barriers and drawn fences (`L8Gates.ts`). Plaza doors (W, E, S) shut at the seal (courier and whole group inside the plaza cell after the 30 s hold; 75 s cap for a straggler; marker "Back to the plaza" until then; checkpoint `sealed`). The pond's north door stays open until the seal (the opening tunnel runners and the K9 bite need it). Fights start when the courier steps into the enclosure zone (elephant: 8 m inside the yard N gate, so his entrance at (89,42) is off-screen). The entry gate closes behind the group when every follower is inside, or after 20 s when nobody is within 9 m of the gate (45 s hard cap, never on a follower in the doorway); the group's hold point is clamped inside the enclosure. Exit opens on the clear with checkpoint `cleared-<id>` at the gate and a keeper line (Nadia, else a soldier). Engage checkpoint is taken in phase 0 before the gate closes. Arena carriers come only from tunnels with a real nav path to the animal, and animals nobody could reach turn 2.5 s after the courier is among them. Waves release only from outlets in the courier's current region, plus one walk-in cohort the first time each region (north path, south field, east lane) is crossed.
- **Plaza hatches (orchestrator decision 11-11)**: two new `kit.zoo-tunnel-mouth` service hatches INSIDE the sealed plaza cell (doors at (-10.4,-5) and (10.4,-5), 20.8 m apart, facing the ring), so "Protect the group!" is a real fight: 3 (low) / 5 (high) runners from each at the breach plus bursts of 2/3 each at 12 s and 24 s of the hold, and while the courier camps in the sealed plaza before the flamingo fight a runner per hatch every 40 s (max 6 inside the cell). The breach releases only tunnels whose runners can really walk to the courier (nav path): the west and east cohorts are no longer released at the breach (they stood harmless against hedges); they walk in when the courier first crosses their region.
- **Guidance**: marker `l8-route` walks per-leg nodes with the plan's texts ("Take the service path to the lion rocks" ...); 12 new dialogue lines (`L8.route-*` plus `.soldier`).
- **Bots/tests/fixtures/docs**: `LevelEightBot` returns to the plaza until the seal and walks the east-lane nodes; `tests/helpers/l8Route.ts` (per gate: followers start 4-8 m outside, the entry gate must shut with nobody left outside, exit opens on clear; run inside the existing missions test); `l8Finale` fixture and the vehicles marker assertion updated; `tools/qa/l8-route-gates.ts` rewritten as a runner proof; `tests/fixtures/saves/qa/l8-*` regenerated (78-181 KB); story-script.md, boss-and-alpha-moves.md, level-acceptance.md (L8-07 reworded) updated.

## Results
- **Flood fill** (`python3 tools/qa/diag/r33-l8route-flood.py $PWD`, real colliders + runtime doors): PASS. Only the next arena is reachable at each stage; the yard opens only after the gorilla. New area per stage: 3,876 m2 (zebra to gorilla), 7,059 m2 (gorilla to yard), as planned.
- **Runner proof** (`route-gates.json`): from the plaza with k arenas cleared, a runner walking 90 s toward the yard enters no later enclosure and never the yard (k < 5); with all five cleared it reaches the yard.
- **Skilled bot, seeds 1-6** (`l8idle-all.sh`, final code): all finish, 370 / 341 / 373 / 345 / 342 / 374 s (main seed 1: 600 s), worst idle 1.4-2.1 s. Engage order flamingo, lion, monkey, zebra, gorilla, elephant.
- **Monkey wait (explained)**: the old 140 s was a metric artifact. `l8carrier.ts` started the clock when the courier came within 12 m of the arena entry, and the calm tour walks past the monkey entry at (-24,4) about 50 s into the level. It now starts the clock when the courier is inside the enclosure zone: lion 5-6 s, monkey 5 s, zebra 5 s, gorilla 5-6 s, elephant 6-37 s (the elephant waits for Hale to be at the door; unchanged rule). The monkey carrier cannot reach the yard through the closed gates, so the 2.5 s fallback turns the pack when the courier is among them (arena engages 5 s after entry).
- **Engine tests**: `missions.test.ts` (incl. checkL8Story and the per-gate test), `vehicles.test.ts`, `navigation`, `npcs`, `spawn-director` pass. `checkL8Cues` now runs its idle hold in god mode (the new opening fight would otherwise kill the idle courier).
- **Threat audit** (`node --import tsx/esm tools/qa/threat-audit.ts --levels L8`, this Mac, final code): skilled complete 365 s, 0 passive flags, the only flag is the diagnostic `PARTIAL_LEVEL_SELECTION`. Breach: 16 runners from 5 tunnels (3, 0, 4, 3, 3, 3), first attack on the courier at 3.8 s; kiting retreat PASS. Main on this Mac: 3 SLOW_FOLLOWER flags + FOLLOWER_WAIT_UPPER_BOUND (all gone).
- **Scene gate, full** (`SCENE_PORT=4918 npm run scene:gate`): 54/62 rows pass; the 8 failures are exactly night-boss-lion and night-boss-zoo-lineup (4 each, known red on main). Nothing else failed.
- **Real play** (`play/`, l8-before-lion, low, zoom 1.5, god, follow-marker, 330 s): service path, lion fight, `cleared-lion` checkpoint, leg to the primate yard. Opened s000, s008, s014, s022: jersey barrier + hedge line across the north path, keeper fence at the lion gate, the group with the courier, marker text per leg. Only one arena fight and the start of the next leg were played, not two full arenas. No main-vs-branch capture of the rebuilt `D-ZOO.base.glb` was taken (it renders fine in the frames above; its size is 1.0 MB vs 1.26 MB on main because the build chain re-compresses it, same as an unchanged rebuild).
