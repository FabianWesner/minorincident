# L4-S1 systems visual review

Headless Scene Lab, game camera, real L4 soldiers, Alpha definition/bar/moves and E26 rail. Desktop 1280×720 high and phone 390×844 low; phone overview zoom fits the fixture. The isolated scenes prove combat behavior, rather than grading the complete war composition or campaign Alpha spawn schedule owned by the other lanes.

- **Soldier focus:** [before](before/qa-l4-systems-focus-high/shot-0027.png) shows both rifles firing at the nearer regular infected; [after](after/qa-l4-systems-focus-high/shot-0027.png) shows both tracers converging on the Alpha. The closer runner retains full HP. At frame 240 both soldiers have fired only at the Alpha from tick 2 to 218 (3.6 s), with the runner still unharmed; no soldier is removed or protected by this implementation.
- **Civilian obstruction:** [before](before/qa-l4-systems-block-high/shot-0027.png) shows the tracer going through the civilian toward the Alpha. [after](after/qa-l4-systems-block-high/shot-0027.png) has the soldier aiming but no shot or damage; Alpha remains 300 HP, civilian remains 100 HP. At [frame 147](after/qa-l4-systems-block-high/shot-0147.png) the ordinary civilian run clears the segment and the Alpha is damaged. Acquisition still sees the Alpha; the civilian prevents firing, rather than hiding the threat.
- **Physical line break and gap:** [frame 27](after/qa-l4-systems-gap-high/shot-0027.png) shows the braced rail with the Alpha charge telegraph. At [frame 90](after/qa-l4-systems-gap-high/shot-0090.png) the brace is gone. The shared resolver logs `barricade.damaged` at tick 68: 300 → 0 HP, Alpha source, stable slot ID, followed by `barricade.broken`. [Frame 450](after/qa-l4-systems-gap-high/shot-0450.png) shows all three ordinary infected on the defended side, with soldiers still facing the Alpha. Both soldiers shoot it from tick 69 to 429 (6 s), while the runners cross from x=7 to x<0. No scripted deletion is used; the existing E26 break removes the collision/navigation blocker and view brace.
- **Knocked bodies:** [before](before/qa-l4-systems-prone-high/shot-0087.png) uses the first systems commit (752dcf24): the Alpha slam knocks the foreground fighter down in the simulation, but the firing story pose stays upright and the rear rifle is blocked. [After](after/qa-l4-systems-prone-high/shot-0087.png) uses the existing fall pose and allows the rifle over the body; the foreground civilian stays at 55 HP, with no friendly damage. The Node check also proves that a low shot still intersects this body and that standing up restores obstruction. The shared Alpha resolver is unchanged.
- **Phone:** [focus](after/qa-l4-systems-focus-low/shot-0027.png), [blocking](after/qa-l4-systems-block-low/shot-0027.png), [gap](after/qa-l4-systems-gap-low/shot-0450.png). The shared health bar remains a contrasting white-bordered red strip; the full fixture uses a wider overview at the game camera angle. This is fixture readability, not a replacement for campaign phone playthrough review.

Behavior review: focus 8/10, shot obstruction 8/10, physical breach/gap 8/10, prone-body clearance 8/10. Ground and actor silhouettes are readable, the breach has visible before/after brace states, and the courier remains identifiable. Known shared rendering defect: the Alpha currently uses a spiky oversized mesh instead of the shipped human Alpha; this is a P1 for character appearance, reported to the separately assigned alpha-render-perf lane. `CrowdView.ts` and Alpha move resolvers are untouched here; these captures do not claim that P1 is fixed.

Reproduce: `SCENE_PORT=3457 E2E_SLOTS=2 sh tools/e2e-lock.sh npx tsx tools/l4/systems-capture.ts after high` (or `after low`). Fixtures are under `tests/scenes/l4-systems/`; nonvisual browser checks run on Hetzner and enforce the three-second focus, human obstruction, logged HP breach and three crossings on both tiers.
