## PO verdict and limits

**Stunning:** the checkpoint lighting and plant silhouettes have a clear night identity; the finale is legible at both requested zooms. The whole route is not consistently stunning.

**Boring:** the patrol bypass is fixed and physically verified. Large shortcut fields are still visually sparse. The actual unprotected shortcut/Resume route waits **about 158 s after charge completion before its first drawn Mega observation**, with an **80.63 s** longest no-progress interval. The ledger separately confirms the same 25 m arena predicate at **218–219 s after activation**, failing L10-11’s unchanged 120–160 s range. This route is too quiet for continuous action; the bands do communicate approach. No arrival target, pursuit relocation or timer was introduced to shorten that wait.

**Babysitting:** no escort-micromanagement defect was established in L10. The protected corgi follows. Console charge pauses outside its radius and clearly asks the player to return. Protected play does not establish human difficulty or unfair-death rates.

**Still open: P2 original perf11 83.4 ms hitch attribution and P2 physical shortcut/Resume L10-11 arrival failure (218–219 s); P3 sparse fields and dense corpse presentation.** The snapshot optimization is a verified reduction in synchronous work; timing proximity alone does not identify the original frame cause. The production comparison passes the inherited maximum rules but does not reproduce the original outlier. The original workload also fails to reproduce the 83.4 ms outlier; its baseline and candidate completion windows are both 16.8 ms. A separate CPU profile and matching 60 s duration replay also fail to reproduce it. Next investigation needs a trace of a genuinely failing frame; the existing replay trace cannot be assigned retrospectively to the original outlier. The 11 incomplete acceptance rows require fresh criterion-specific browser/manual evidence; these PO spot checks do not replace those reviews. Personal audio listening and PO taste approval remain human checks. Specifications, acceptance targets, and L1/L2/L4/L5 tuning were not changed.
