# L3 Boulevard browser repair

The starting production build reproduced T-E21-19 with 18 Boulevard deaths. The real-input route and its five-death limit are unchanged.

## Causes and changes

- Returning infected retain director slots. When a fatal bite could not turn an officer at that cap, `Outbreak.kill` marked the officer finished but left HP at 100. Those dead officers still blocked allied fire and counted as living rescue members. Fatal bites now zero allied-fighter HP while retaining the same corpse entity and rendering.
- Ground clicks during an in-place swing returned before movement handling and before ground-button suppression. They lost the move order and could buffer another axe attack. The existing move target now retains that command, and a ground click cannot become another attack. Holding Shift still plants the courier for the full swing. A regression supplies the real mouse-frame flags and checks movement resumes with exactly one attack.
- Melee aim assist accepted any short ground click in a 35-degree cone, including a click well before an infected. It now assists only near the target's feet. A real-mouse regression checks both clear-ground movement and a valid near-foot target click. The driver checks the sampled move command instead of an empty pick diagnostic, which cannot see aim assist; its waypoint list, candidate clicks, arrival tolerances and death limit are unchanged.
- Returning crowds repeatedly caught the courier at the same narrow passages. The existing sandbag pallet moves from (31,-23.4) to (38,-32.5) beside the wrecks; the school bus at x=66 shifts south from z=-28.2 to -29.5; the police sedan at x=84 shifts south from z=-26.2 to -28.5. These retain the same dressing and solids while leaving room along the north sidewalk to pass and sidestep.
- Original population plans, arrival budgets, speeds, sprinter ratio, recovery times, collision resistance and medkit positions are retained. Speed, arrival and medkit experiments were removed after their browser and bot results failed the gates.
- Retry relief for repeated deaths increases from 30%/75% to 50%/90%. The original delay before thinning standing threats is retained: an attempted first-retry change violated checkpoint outbreak restoration and was rolled back after T-E21-18 exposed it. Downed bodies are excluded, retaining their entities, slots and get-up timers; the new regression checks all three on the second retry.
- L3 nearby threats now retain their existing respawn loss-of-target state for 20 seconds, covering the human recovery window. The checkpoint location, two-second invulnerability, body identity, HP restoration and recovery timestamp are unchanged; other levels retain four seconds. A regression respawns five seconds before a body stands and checks the same entity recovers while still ignoring the courier. This reduced repeated immediate reacquisition but did not make the final browser route pass.
- Squad close-range accuracy increases from 85% to 90% in S1 only to retain the opening's police cover; later streets restore 85%. Damage remains at 50%; cadence, range and distant accuracy are unchanged.
- The HMG post at (220,-7.2) had no clear shot to sampled centre-lane targets at x=210,215,216,218,220. Its anchor moves to (221.5,-1.5) in the inner lane. The existing nest moves clear of the watchtower; soldiers prioritize visible compound breaches while retaining range, cover and friendly-fire checks.
- Rescued officers near the compound edge were ordered back west after boom closure, reopening it and cancelling `crossedAt`. Their regroup order now continues inward. Clearing a healthy bystander also relocates its standing order so it cannot immediately return under the arm.
- The occupied-boom browser test paused the world and advanced only 1.5 seconds before waiting for L4. The PO combat outro lasts five seconds. Its final step now advances the existing closure/outro duration; no route, limit or assertion changed.

## Visual review

Catalog scenes: `l3-blvd-returning`, `l3-axe-escape`, `l3-blvd-wreck-passage`, `l3-army-inner-cover`, `l3-army-occupied-boom`. The matrix covers high/low quality and zoom 1.5/1/0.5 on WebGL2 and WebGPU. Production captures use the normal complete policy with god mode solely to isolate visual QA; survival is measured by the real-input test.

Inspected Boulevard game-camera captures at high/low quality and zoom 1/0.5: the sidewalk is distinct from the road and building cutaways expose the walking line. At zoom 1 the courier and nearby attackers are readable; at zoom 0.5 the view is close enough to crop nearby actors. In the low-quality capture the companion occupies the foreground and the courier is partly beyond the upper edge, a camera-composition limitation retained in this change. Retained bodies lie on the support and the returning-body sheets show them standing again in place. Low quality has visibly coarse vehicle geometry; no vehicle meshes or LOD assets are edited in this repair. Army sheets show the downed breach remaining in place, the arm clear of the courier, and a readable compound lane. The axe sheets show movement resuming after the planted swing. Passage sheets show the courier traversing the full north sidewalk; at the final sample the near building can obscure the courier, another existing cutaway/composition limitation. WebGL2 and WebGPU look consistent in the inspected sheets.

Artifacts are under `test-results/l3-blvd-browser/` and `test-results/epics/E21/`. Final validation results are recorded in the lane report.
