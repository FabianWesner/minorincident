# Q2 game-camera review

Captured from a fresh production build in headless Chromium on the Mac's native Metal GPU. Scene Lab asserts the requested backend; these runs used WebGL2 and WebGPU, each at high/low quality and zoom 0.5/1.0/1.5. The seven catalog scenes passed all 84 configurations. Raw measurements are in `matrix-{webgl2,webgpu}.json`, `opening-{webgl2,webgpu}.json` and `terrain-{webgl2,webgpu}.json`. Twenty-eight representative game-camera sheets were opened and inspected: high/1.0 and low/0.5 for each scene on each backend. This is focused inspection of the changed systems, not the full AC18 vision/photospot sign-off.

The handover scenes retain L9's crawl presentation after its completion event. The supplemental axe-opening scene isolates the standing end composition; it does not claim to execute a real L10 transition. Existing `l9-handover` sim/browser tests and the new campaign assert the real inventory/transport boundary. No visual asset or camera implementation was changed in this lane.

Scoped assessment: body/ground contact, damage continuity, staged fire and backend/tier parity are acceptable in these captures. Existing close-camera framing/foliage issues are recorded above. Full touch/mouse playtests, every required photo pair, vision scoring and real-device performance remain with Q3/Q4 as mapped in [coverage.md](coverage.md).

## Real Game handover capture

The existing browser handover test also captured actual L10 after the crash on both tiers, then verified reload. [High start](captures/real-l10-high-start.png) and [low start](captures/real-l10-low-start.png) were opened: the courier stands with the red fire axe and backpack beside the tongue-out corgi, with no old gun drawn. Ground contact and shadows are clear against the dark road. High shows the 100/100 normal spawn-health HUD and axe action icon; the narrow low viewport keeps health/corgi/objective text readable, but the Home button overlaps the minimap in its upper-left corner. This unchanged HUD arrangement is a Q3 follow-up; this capture is not a clean mobile HUD sign-off. Background truck and pavement differ from the isolated composition, confirming this is the real next-level view.

Opening the first inherited D2 terrain sheets exposed a fixture error: their landmark cameras framed buildings, leaving the APC out of view. Both new Q2 floor scenes now explicitly follow `apc`; both complete backend/tier/zoom matrices were rerun and the corrected sheets inspected. The wheel/contact and render gates are unchanged.
