# Game-camera review

Reviewed the 32 required catalog contact sheets (four fixtures × WebGL/WebGPU × high/low × zoom 1/0.5); the extra zoom-1.5 cases also passed their numeric gates. Saved sheets are in [views/webgl2](views/webgl2/) and [views/webgpu](views/webgpu/). The catalog uses the production renderer and checks the requested backend and quality; WebGPU did not fall back to WebGL.

The first WebGL catalog run passed numeric gates but its inherited fixed Northgate/corridor cameras cropped the courier at close zoom. The new catalog wrappers now follow the courier during the approach. The final matrices preserve the inherited foot, facing, draw, triangle and console gates. No runtime camera or visual assets changed.

- **Northgate:** the courier stays in frame while walking west down the raised exit apron. The army truck and green rocket glow are visible beside the starting position. The road is dark blue/purple, with warm fire spill near the lower edge; the orange/teal courier silhouette stays distinct. At zoom 0.5 the truck leaves the crop as the camera follows the walk, which matches player camera behavior.
- **Rocket:** the launcher glows beside the wreck, then moves into the courier's hands during the normal interaction. The close views show the held green tube and grip clearly. The truck is dark and partially cropped at close zoom; the pickup itself is distinguishable from the paving.
- **Corridor:** the courier follows the open lane between wrecks, barriers, paving and fires. Follow framing corrects the original off-screen-subject problem. Warm pools and cool road/shadows remain consistent across the backends; low quality reduces detail and some shadow cues without losing the courier or route.
- **Bus:** six figures gather and board at the lit door, then the bus travels along the road past fires/smoke and street lamps. The camera switches to its authored departure shot, and the final black panel is the scripted fade. The shot is an isolated corridor fixture and does not prove the whole-city skyline area criterion.

Opened the production handover/retry captures and saved eight labelled comparison strips in [views/production](views/production/). The L6 apron setup produces a real result/Continue flow carrying 29 survivors, followed by the L7 night exit and actual rocket/corridor retries. The red courier, teal pack and real corgi are clear in the corridor retry. At the exit, the 29-person formation and many health bars overlap the courier substantially, especially at zoom 0.5. Pale polygon-edge highlights on carried clothing are conspicuous on both renderers. One WebGPU low/0.5 L6 result capture is black; its later L7 frames render normally. These captures do not establish a whole-level vision pass, numeric 3:1 contrast, or a passing full L6 route.

The separate unchanged full-route requirements still fail before handover: the courier stalls below the raised exit near x=255,z=15 and the mission remains playing after the 3500-iteration limit. Its failure is retained in the report; the apron fixture is explicitly a focused handover test.
