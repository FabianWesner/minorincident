# s196 — L10 corgi hangar entry

## Finding

The hangar opening was 2 m wide, formed by the west/east door wall colliders in `src/levels/L10/layout.ts`. NavGrid had a route through the doorway, but the corgi's elongated collision footprint turned into the side of the opening and stalled outside (a native HIGH reproduction stopped at z=52.26 against the z=52.6 wall face). The soldiers are not holding the corgi, and the follow system has no hangar hold. The ordinary 2 m following band also lets a dog settle before it clears the boundary when the courier pauses just inside.

## Change

Widened the hangar's clear entrance to 4 m. While the courier is inside the hangar and the corgi is still outside, the follower continues toward the centered entrance/interior target with a 0.1 m stop distance until its full 0.84 m footprint clears the boundary. This uses ordinary navigation and collision movement; it does not relocate the corgi.

Folded the regression into the existing `tests/engine/npcs.test.ts` case. HIGH and LOW each cover a synthetic `cure-research-entry` checkpoint captured at the doorway, its actual restore, a courier walking the final approach into the hangar, and approaches from both sides of the apron. After restore the test reacquires the corgi entity, since restore replaces its snapshot. The corgi clears the hangar boundary by 0.84 m within 15 seconds and stays within 6 m of the courier.
