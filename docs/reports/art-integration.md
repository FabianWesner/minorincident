# Production art integration · E17 / E02

136 registered optimized exports are integrated (135 promotions). All accepted tracked runtime exports are enabled; no Blender sources were rebuilt. The 22 tracked raw models without registered optimized exports remain pending. Six older world building IDs resolve to matching accepted production assets; they are excluded from `still_placeholder` in the JSON report. Other pending entries include code icons and effects as well as missing models.

The runtime now uses accepted survivor rigs at their manifest height, the corgi and authored mission actors, actual weapons/pickups/vehicles, and instanced infected with real LOD0/LOD1/LOD2. Static placements fold diffuse swatches into vertex colors and retain separate powered windows; static LOD0 streams only for visible placements within 12m of the camera target, LOD1 covers up to 30m, and LOD2 covers farther placements. Infected use LOD1/LOD2 except the nearest eight visible living infected, whose LOD0 loads lazily. All use camera-frustum culling. Quantized attributes are decoded before transforms; shared vertices and powered/unpowered diffuse geometry are retained to reduce heap usage. Mission actors and accepted vehicle-course obstacles move rigidly and share static production prototypes; physics-controlled vehicles retain their wheel and feedback hierarchy while changing LOD. Low quality uses survivor/corgi LOD1, crowd LOD2 only, and skips distant static shadow draws. Empty crowd batches remain hidden during warm-up.

Survivor material primitives merge within each animated joint while preserving the authored pivots, sockets, custom colors and blood shading. The low survivor drops from 87 to roughly 23 draws per pass without reducing triangles. Static building aliases fit the legacy X/Z footprint while retaining authored Y heights so doors and floors remain human sized. Actual production bounds drive frustum culling.

The corgi presentation is cosmetic in this checkout; companion gameplay remains owned by its separate epic. Physics-controlled cars remain owned by VehicleView and are excluded from the mission actor layer.
