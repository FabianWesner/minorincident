# L9-S4 residency

Scope: the S4 row of E31, with PO 2026-10-13 as the source of truth. No normative spec, reference art, static collision asset table, or asset geometry changed. The Scene Lab catalog additions follow the requested quality × zoom gate.

`SimWorld.chunks` owns the existing S3 predictive 100 m chunk selection independently of visual downloads. Static collider handles are shared across intersected resident cells and removed when their final owner unloads. The existing finite coarse ground remains available. Active AI navigation uses one bounded window of the existing NavGrid; its rectangular occupancy bake and wall traversal preserve the clearance/sweep decisions while avoiding work on every empty terrain cell. The Mega receives a window at its full body clearance and follows the same residency query. Its route planning stays at 2 Hz and coarse position advances every tick.

Inactive infected, corpses, pedestrians, pickups and movable props retain numeric IDs, HP, component state and remaining timers as JSON chunk records. Prop bodies/nav blockers are released and rebuilt from the existing pose snapshot/spawner. Low quality queues excess brains without changing their HP or IDs; high quality restores them. The mission vehicle and protected companion retain their existing owners. Checkpoint seams and L9 campaign saves include the chunk records. Timer adjustment occurs once on wake, including saves resumed in a new tick-zero world; consumed health pickups remain consumed.

Inspection: `__SS__.getState().chunks`, `getState().chunkResidency`, and `perf().chunkResidency`. Counts expose resident, queued, unloaded, inactive records, static colliders and nav cells. The class accepts global chunk bounds and uses the existing lifecycle, snapshot and quality hooks as the shared E32 basis.
