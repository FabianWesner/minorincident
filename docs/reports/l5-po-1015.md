Inspection of the production source found that trigger and the independent heartbeat path. The PO's exact 20 wall-second onset was not independently reproduced.

Navigation, reachability, Rapier capsule overlap and live infected occupancy admit the exit spot; a temporarily blocked door is retried. Legacy saves fall back to the actual cellar door. L5 has one re-enterable safe-space door; the Metro is a one-way campaign handover.
