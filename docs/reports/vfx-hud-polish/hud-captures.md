## L5 first presentation and emergence

Each sheet contains the cellar and first emergence pose at all three zooms. The courier and corgi stay centred and visible; the cellar walls, barrels and warm lighting remain stable before the cut, and the sidewalk/red façade replace them immediately after emergence. Low simplifies the distant yard/shadows. High and low agree on both backends. The outward zoom can contract near buildings due to existing camera clearance.
