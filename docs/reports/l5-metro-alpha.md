# L5 Metro Alpha and shutter regression

The Metro-side Alpha spawned at (49, -22), about 30 m from the shutter and only 16 m from Metro Road. L5 assigned a 14 m leash only to `infected.l1`; Alphas have no L1 brain, so investigation, pursuit and charge were unconstrained. The courier could pull that Alpha onto the final escort leg. Followers crouched for an infected within 8 m before checking Metro ingress, preventing all living followers from crossing and therefore preventing closure. The open shutter also remained passable throughout its two-second descent.

The Alpha now spawns at (49, 14), in a town encounter. Both authored Alphas retain a checkpointed 14 m territory, including investigation, pursuit and charge movement. Their entire encounter circles stay more than 25 m from the entrance and clear of the final Metro Road escort leg. Town combat, Alpha HP, shotgun damage and the sparse infected count are unchanged.

Metro ingress takes priority over crouching. Closure still requires the courier and every living follower to cross. At closure onset the shutter blocks navigation and sweeps infected in its opening/shaft out to the street, cancelling active specials. Its existing visible descent, closed gate and campaign handover remain.
