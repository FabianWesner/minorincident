Opened the time-sequence sheets and the combined [WebGL2](webgl2-matrix.jpg) and [WebGPU](webgpu-matrix.jpg) comparisons. The red-orange boundaries remain readable on asphalt on both quality tiers. The lane has small open chevrons, visible forward motion and an increasing fill instead of solid yellow arrowheads. At zoom 0.5 the road cracks, shadows and feet remain visible through the band. At zoom 1 and 1.5 the lane remains continuous and its direction is apparent. Slam uses a thin danger ring and expanding translucent interior, keeping the courier visible. Grab marks the throw corridor while the Alpha lifts its retained body. The game camera naturally crops parts of large areas at close zoom; the visible band still communicates danger. Markers disappear at activation, with the existing attack animation continuing.

The visual regression formerly required an unchanging pixel image. It now requires immediate/readable warning pixels, a fixed ground footprint within two pixels, moving lane chevrons, persistence and resolution. The unrelated cross retains its original static-image check. No gameplay or performance budgets were loosened.
