# L8 continuity QA fixes

Base: `93e1fbea8` (`lane/l7-departure-p0`, `020b5b92f`).

The campaign now selects the courier outfit through L10 for both character variants. The existing sim/campaign variant and gear tier drive `CharacterView`. Cosmetic tier calculation uses the crash record's existing weapon-loss history, so L10's mandatory axe-only reset retains the earned tier (the default campaign previously fell from 4 to 2). Combat power and usable weapons still use only the current arsenal; render diagnostics expose the actual visible gear groups as `gearMeshes`. The shipped tier 0–4 armor groups are empty (the unfitted placeholder armor was removed previously). This fix preserves that state, the cap, yellow/orange top, teal bag and courier shoes. L6's authored headlamp and L10's later cure item remain mission attachments.

L7's stalled-follower relocation now requires the entire silhouette to be outside the director's camera frustum, including its existing 10% margin, and farther than 12 m from the courier. Visible or nearby followers retry navigation. An unsuccessful retry, or another stall after a successful repath, escalates to a continuous scripted step-through at ordinary follower speed (3.8 m/s, injured 1.4 m/s). This uses the bus's existing scripted passenger ownership while crossing the obstruction; the actor stays shown until it crosses the actual door. It never drops the survivor or counts it aboard early. Assistance serializes in departure state and resumes after checkpoint restore. `l7.survivor-assisted` distinguishes visible assistance from `l7.survivor-recovered` hidden relocation.

The 3D objective ring/arrow and the projected objective arrow hide during departure, story locks, cinematic/photo presets and the camera's blend back to play. HUD and MissionUI reset their objective text on load, and the HUD clears it whenever there is no current/completed objective. L8's calm arrival therefore carries no stale bus objective. Level camera initialization retains the requested player zoom, clamps it to the shared allowed range (0.5–1.5), and clears temporary story/vehicle framing. Telemetry records the initial zoom and the replay driver restores it before consuming frames, since camera visibility affects spawning; older logs keep the former initial default (or the saved L10 resume zoom). Scene Lab already applies authored zoom absolutely.
