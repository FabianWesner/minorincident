# r37-l4north: heavy encounters on the L4 north street

PO: "tons of" tough zombies, and encounters on the street north. Zombies unchanged (no stat tuning); only placement, mix and count.

## What the "street north" is
Escape phase (`hide`, after "Fall back!"). The courier (and the sim bot) leaves the plaza north and runs Harbor Street west (z about -38) to the refuge at (-79, -45); the marker's loop goes east via Lakeshore and Metro Road (x 48.5) north, then Harbor Street west. Existing collapse groups there were small, mostly ordinary infected (arrival roster, one rotating heavy).

## Change (data in `src/data/l4.ts`, mechanics in `LevelFourWar.ts`)
Three new authored encounters appended to `l4CollapseGroups` (indices 18..20, anchors generated by the layout as before). A 5th tuple element selects the kind.

| # | Position | Kind | High / low count |
|---|---|---|---|
| 18 | (-48, -39) Harbor Street west, 30 m from the plaza exit | heavy mix | 5 / 3 |
| 19 | (-66, -40.2) Harbor Street far west, wide crossing | Alpha + heavy mix | 4 / 2 |
| 20 | (48.5, -14) Metro Road north (marker loop) | heavy mix | 5 / 3 |

- Heavy mix roster (`l4EncounterRoster`): brute, armored, butcher, riot, firefighter, hazmat, runner, balanced least-represented-first, so 2-3 heavies per group of 5.
- The Alpha is a normal L4-mix Alpha (`alphaTypeFor('L4')`), spawned outside `alphaIds` so the war jobs do not script it. It keeps the existing amber windup rings.
- Same emergence rules as other street groups: unseen, 24 m or more from the courier, group waits at its home looking along the street and engages by sight. They are in `pendingDoor`, so the collapse retreat/pull logic leaves them alone.
- Fix needed to make them appear: the war survivors fill the 120 cap and the existing reservation maths (reserved 59) gave room 0, so no authored group could emerge for a courier who is not killing. Authored encounters now draw on the live cap directly (`emergenceRoom(..., authored)`), with a fallback that retires one far, idle, unseen, non-authored regular (never an Alpha, never a corpse) if the cap is full. They reserve no slots, so the hold is not affected.
