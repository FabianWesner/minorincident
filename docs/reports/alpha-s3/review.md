# A-S3: Alpha Grab and throw / Roar

Implementation uses the existing Alpha scheduler, PropThrows, retained corpse poses, E26 medium bodies, hearing/LOS, and steering. Grab locks the last-seen target, selects the nearest available object within 2 m, sweeps at 12 m/s up to 15 m, and applies one 30-damage / 0.8-second knockdown. Roar supplies its origin to regular human infected within 18 m, applies one 1.25 speed scale for 300 ticks, and emits expiry without changing base speed. Cooldowns, flight progress, and buffs survive mission checkpoint restore. Alpha and zoo animals are excluded. Charge and Ground slam remain owned by A-S2. The renderer retires cues synchronously on typed special-stage events, including paused Scene Lab stepping before the next render frame.
