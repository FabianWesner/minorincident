## Requirements audit, current source

- **Gameplay:** The changes since `3d9e12b1` are limited to render code, Scene Lab tooling/catalog and this report. No sim, weapon balance, fuel, range or cone source changed. The remote suite passed on `1cc54812` (the latest render source); this supports but does not independently prove every gameplay scenario.
- **Body:** Fixed slots retain each emission pose and travel, spread, rise and grow; the flame shader uses animated per-card noise and colors from hot yellow through orange to red. The coherent 8 Hz reach factor ranges from 0.8 to 1.0 of the clipped ray. Captures show a changing silhouette, but the first frame shows the visual arriving after instantaneous damage.
- **Embers, smoke and impact:** Shared-pool sparks and rising smoke are emitted in both tiers, and the clipped endpoint has three upright splash cards. Fight and wall frames show the stream and endpoint; their framing is too wide to judge ember trajectories and the complete cooling-to-smoke transition conclusively. Infected impact uses existing gameplay fire and is visible in the fight sheet.
- **Light:** Two flickering light-field footprints track nozzle and stream. The night probe shows warm spill on the ground and palette materials sample this field; the courier contribution is subtle. They are not Three.js point lights. Whether the requested point-light appearance is met on the courier remains a visual review item.
- **Press/release:** A muzzle flash is spawned on a new firing sequence; release begins a 0.24 s scattered fade. The lifecycle probe observed a tail and a second firing event; the inspected release frame showed faint broken tongues. The ignition flash itself has not been isolated in a capture.
- **Budget/backends:** Slots are fixed at 32 HIGH / 24 LOW plus three splash cards in the existing 128-card pool; smoke and sparks use the existing shared particle pool. The flame-specific trace direction, light handles, writer closures and combined shadow array were made reusable. Iteration and other shared renderer paths have not been heap-profiled, so the absolute no-per-frame-allocation claim is not proven. Headless WebGL2 and WebGPU scene captures were inspected, with no captured console errors; the latest WebGPU check predates the reach-scale correction, and interactive manual WebGPU review remains open.
- **Capture/perf/checks:** The linked sheet contains six HIGH frames at 0.1 s intervals and one LOW frame at zoom 1.5, against three infected. The latest matched frame counts are 62 → 62 draws and 15 → 27 shared particles HIGH; 56 → 56 draws and 5 → 11 particles LOW. Timing is pending as requested. Typecheck, lint and the full remote unit suite passed on the latest render source. Orchestrator review of the sheet remains external.

Allocation call-chain audit: flame slot storage, smoke/spark spawning and the writer's explicit scratch objects are reused. However, `Vfx.flameRange` calls the existing `HitQuery.clearDistance` and `Destruction.trace` during refresh. For rotated cover, `HitQuery.cover` creates transformed objects; `Destruction.trace` constructs arrays and hit records while querying destructible parts. Thus an absolute zero-allocation claim for the full firing refresh is contradicted by source inspection in scenes containing those objects. Eliminating those allocations would require a separate allocation-free query path with equivalence checks; no sim query was changed here. The orchestrator has been notified of the capture sheet and its visible startup gap and subtle smoke.
