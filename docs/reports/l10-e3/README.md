# L10-E3 finale encounters

This lane implements the charge-triggered mixed assault on the existing LevelTenEncounters ledger and connects the existing MegaPursuit to the production L10 composition. It does **not** establish the end-to-end timing or arrival targets: the measured route is shorter than the epic's allocated time, and the pursuing boss arrives late on that route. No teleport, replacement boss, padded wait, changed boss speed, changed rocket damage, or relaxed budget was added to make that measurement pass.

The assault has 160/80 regulars, four dogs, and 3/2 regular Alphas at high/low quality. The persistent Mega gives total authored peaks of 168/87. The existing global 200/100 cap, dormant-boss reservation, pending conversions and pool availability govern admission. When overlap leaves few slots, dogs and Alphas are admitted before regulars; blocked/full slots remain deferred in the stable ledger. Activation starts chase immediately, including a directly loaded ready checkpoint. Release disables further admission. Low quality thins the regular crowd and uses the existing R7 Alpha quota.

The ring of approach positions surrounds the hold zone; the shared bounded search avoids industrial props and off-screen spawning. R1–R6 retain their existing 17-attempt placement search; the arena uses up to 129 attempts. Scene Lab uses the same search and assault positions, plus chase state, instead of independently duplicating placement geometry.

Production previously never installed MegaPursuit. It now installs that existing component with the authored shared route, its usual 90 s introduction, and an entrance point 80 m behind the first route node. Its reserved slot, stable ID, off-region movement, checkpoint state, damage floor, specials and corpse identity remain owned by the existing systems. The assault never schedules a boss entrance or moves the boss.

Corpses retain their entity IDs and positions. Existing settlement keeps a frozen body record while recycling the simulated brain; existing chunk snapshots retain that body across unload/revisit. Neither the assault nor its cap admission deletes a corpse. The saturation tests kill twelve incoming actors, replenish the freed living slots, revisit the arena, and resume a save, with all twelve bodies retained.

## Evidence and limits

`tests/sim/levels/l10-assault.test.ts` covers high/low mixed peaks, one persistent ID, continuous approach, full-cap deferral, corpse/revisit/save persistence, full-HP survival of nine resolver hits and permanent death on the tenth, duplicate projectile rejection, and explicit release. From the authored R7 approach 60 m west of the console, arrival is 12.67/12.73 s. This focused approach fixture is not proof of the default whole-route arrival target. The final seed-32 route reaches release at 486.65 s with 53.3 HP, no deaths, peak 168, ten direct boss rocket hits and arrival 179.1 s after activation. Existing `mega-moves.test.ts` additionally tests real stock rocket projectile contact through Explosions and minigun/conventional floors.

`tools/sim-runner/l10-finale-calibration.ts` uses seed 32 only, starts with the rocket checkpoint's arsenal/mission/position and a pursuing boss 80 m behind, then emits ordinary input through release. It uses no cheats, teleports, HP changes, clock jumps or idle timing padding after initialization. Initial fixture debugging mistakenly left the player at the crash location; that invalid setup was discarded. Only seed 32 is used throughout calibration; no seed battery was run. The report's remaining-route band is inferred from a 29 min checkpoint, 45–50 min total and the default 2.5 min epilogue: **13.5–18.5 min to release**. This does not replace a complete newbie L10 run.

The machine-readable proof files in this directory record the final measured values. Overall lane status remains **incomplete for route timing/arrival**, even if focused rules and render counters pass. Full-level/campaign timing and real-device measurements belong to the follow-up integration lanes; no claim is made for them here.
