## Zebra

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/TheKingOfGeeks360/sounds/850661/
- License on source page: CC0 1.0 (http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Source description: In media such as the online game, "Snorin" and "The Lion Guard", zebras are depicted neighing like a horse when in reality, in the case of the Grant's zebra as heard here, they don't, but rather, they make this bray sound comparable to a small dog bark.

Grant's zebras are a subspecies of common (or plains) zebra and are the smallest of these ornate black-and-white equids. They migrate alongside wildebeests in the Masai Mara migration.

Recorded with an LG Stylus 2022.
- HQ preview SHA-256: `27676ec134bbb99e047872f65d474387f628a5c4a9366a716845c82847403633`
