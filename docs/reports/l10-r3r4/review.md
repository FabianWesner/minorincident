# L10-D2 visual review

Reviewed all eight areas on WebGL2 and WebGPU at high/low, zoom 1.5/1.0/0.5, using the game camera and actual L10 night lighting. Contact sheets are in `views/`. Also reviewed actual browser checkpoint captures after real KeyE shotgun/MG interactions (`views/game-checkpoints.jpg`).

- Depot/post: courier remains immediately identifiable by warm skin/clothing, teal backpack and a soft ground halo. Low quality preserves the same visible module silhouette. Road stripes and barricade/cone spacing remain legible at zoom 1; buildings move outside the close zoom 0.5 field of view.
- Industrial loading dock: the closer forecourt view exposes doors, canopy, bollards and the yellow platform edge in both tiers. Warehouse wall, dock and ground separate clearly. The first view showed the rear wall; the catalog now targets the usable dock.
- Service detour: barrels mark the road edge without blocking the clear paved lane. Dark parallel bands are cast shadows, consistent between tiers/backends. No visible terrain cracks or floating road edges.
- Military breach: Jersey barriers and painted road frame an open passage through the defensive line. The enlarged camera view distinguishes the obstacles; closer zoom emphasizes the courier and lane.
- Motor pool: roof fascia, posts and truck wheels/headlights/body remain visible in both tiers. Inspection found the parked trucks intersecting the 16 cm shelter slab. Trucks were raised to 16.1 cm and the fixture now requires zero clipping; all twelve final recaptures passed with zero static pairs, gun/body, held-prop or actor intersections. Both final sheets were opened; wheels/slab contact and shelter fit are clear.
- Industrial/military seam: painted paved corridor continues across the region boundary, with no visible height break in any matrix cell. Research handoff shows the authored end apron and continuous ground; the next region belongs to D3.
- Actual checkpoint game captures: shotgun and MG are visibly distinct in the courier’s hands; objective text progresses from machine-gun recovery to research-campus travel. Night palette is navy asphalt, subdued green ground, tinted shadows and a warm readable courier. Minimap and objective text are visible. These test-mode captures do not prove the full production HUD or mobile layout.
