# s93-l10finale

The L10 cadence goal remains open. This report records a baseline HIGH seed 3 trace, the diagnosed encounter-flow conditions, and a rejected release-radius experiment. No gameplay change is retained.

## Trace findings

The retained trace is generated by [`tools/qa/s93-l10trace.ts`](../../../tools/qa/s93-l10trace.ts) with the native complete policy and the same attack-start observer used by s89. It records a 6-death completion. The failing gaps are 935.33–1034.58 s (99.25 s) and 1059.17–1229.78 s (170.62 s), outside protected dialogue. See [trace.json](trace.json), a compact extract of the native sample output. Reproduce with `S93_TRACE_SEED=3 S93_TRACE_TIER=high S93_TRACE_OUT=/private/tmp/s93-baseline-trace.json node --import tsx/esm tools/qa/s93-l10trace.ts`.

At cure installation (about 941 s), the persistent Mega is about 1,615 m away. The charge becomes ready around 1005 s while the Mega is still about 1,300 m away. Finale waves do continue to admit at the existing 22 HIGH cap; however, nearby actors cycle through `attracted`, `wander`, `search`, and `stagger`, with few in `chase` and no corresponding player attack starts. During the second quiet interval, the Mega closes from roughly 1,050 m to contact, and the next attack start occurs at 1229.78 s. The per-tick trace logs repeated loud player-gunfire noise events around the same time a focused actor moves from `chase` to `attracted`, where its L1 target is 0 and its search deadline is extended. This suggests noise-driven investigation is interrupting pursuit. However, focused actors had zero transitions matching the separate L10 “outpaced hunter” drop condition during 940–1230 s.

Suppressing gunfire-driven noise reactions for already-focused finale actors was tested and reverted. It completed seed 3 HIGH with 8 deaths, but the longest attack-start gap expanded to 296.48 s (935.33–1231.82 s). The evidence therefore rejects noise suppression as a sufficient fix; the precise root cause remains unresolved. No source change has been shown to close both gaps while preserving all six completions, attack rates, and resident limits.

## Rejected experiment

Reducing the authored group release radius from 120 m to 60 m was tested with all six native seed/tier cases, then reverted. It did not solve the cadence: seed 1 HIGH reached 47.75 s, seed 2 HIGH 87.82 s, seed 3 HIGH 47.28 s, and seed 3 LOW did not complete. The remaining rows completed, but passing individual rows cannot be combined across different experiments. The experiment is not retained.

Earlier explorations (directly retargeting nearby infected, faster assault timing, and catch-up relocation) were also reverted. Retargeting increased deaths without solving the gaps; faster admission did not overcome a full resident cap; relocating the Mega conflicted with E32 AC10, which keeps its pursuit continuous and forbids teleportation.

## Validation

The baseline six-case run is documented in [s89-l10gap](../s89-l10gap/README.md). This report's trace run completed for HIGH seed 3. The release-radius experiment and focused-noise experiment did not pass, so neither is a candidate implementation. `npm run typecheck` and `npm run lint` pass. The L10 threat audit produced only its standard `PARTIAL_LEVEL_SELECTION:diagnostic run, not a complete gate` flag and reported no L10 gameplay flags. The remote wrapper could not run because no Hetzner runner was available. The unchanged suite passed locally with `npm test -- --maxWorkers=4` (107 tests, 27 files, 92.3 s). These checks validate the diagnostic artifact, not a gameplay fix.

## Recovery census follow-up

A fresh native trace includes recoverable downed actors, which were missing from the earlier living-only samples. The director is strict: downed actors retain their admission slots until recovery. At 1000 s, 13 of 22 slots are downed; at 1200 s, 15 are downed, all within about 5–12 m of the courier. The same IDs repeatedly fall and recover while the distant Mega approaches. This explains the full cap despite few standing enemies and rejects the earlier assumption that all occupied slots represented distant living hunters. See `recoveryCensus` in trace.json. A compliant fix must account for recovery without changing infected stats, dropping downed reservations, removing corpses, or raising the finale cap.

A subsequent composition experiment replaced one assault regular with an Alpha and admitted dog/Alpha slots before regulars, retaining the group size and cap. Native HIGH seed 3 completed with 20 deaths; post-install gaps included 77.10, 66.60 and 62.92 s. This reduced the longest finale gap but failed the goal and increased deaths substantially; it was reverted. No gameplay change is retained.

Prioritizing only the existing dog slot, with no composition/count change, also failed: HIGH seed 3 completed with four deaths but a 222.42 s finale gap and 15.481 starts/min (below s89's 17.699). This experiment was reverted.

A four-direction assault entry (adding east to the existing west/north/south ring) was also rejected: HIGH seed 3 completed with 20 deaths and a 292.25 s finale gap. It was reverted; the absent east entry is not a sufficient explanation for the cadence failure.
