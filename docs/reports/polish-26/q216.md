# Q-216 · oversized added hair

The brown dome is an `acc.hair-*` accessory, not a hat. The measured skull retarget included the base character's existing hair depth. Hats had a bare-brow size cap, but added hair did not, producing pieces up to 86.6 cm deep on a roughly 35 cm head. Hair now uses the measured bare brow for horizontal fit and centring, with a 1.2 envelope cap and bounded height. Both LODs contribute to one cached fit.

Viewed the six-style living [before](q216/living-before.png) and [after](q216/living-after.png): the bun, afro and brown crop no longer project far behind the skull or spread over the shoulders. The [corpse before](q216/corpse-before.png) shows a large loose-looking shell beside the middle body; [after](q216/corpse-after.png) it follows the head silhouette. There is no separately dropped hair entity: the corpse freezes the same fitted head piece, so its fit must be corrected at the common source. The ponytail and long hair retain their recognizable forms.

[Before bounds](q216/fits-before.json), [after bounds](q216/fits-after.json): man's afro depth 70.9 → 41.5 cm, ponytail 86.6 → 41.3 cm; woman's crop 55.2 → 35.1 cm. The dedicated living → infected → corpse capture runs 240 frames without console errors. The full scene gate additionally covers headwear and corpse rows.
