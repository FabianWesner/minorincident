# Final cast review

Decision: **PASS for the relative body-build change; QA candidate for the PO’s style review.**

Reviewed the final high/low overview sheets, front/side comparisons and individual groups containing segmented legacy limbs, armour, story survivors, the clerk and children. The narrower torsos, limb middles and shoe footprints move the adult silhouettes toward the heroine while retaining their clothing, uniforms, packs and hand props. Legacy joint ends retain their connecting volume. The Alphas, brute and bloated retain their bulk; the two children retain their q257 proportions and height classes.

The pilot’s final studio front/three-quarter/side pairs and game zoom 1.5 / literal 3 m pairs were reviewed again after the numerical sampling correction. Motion receipts confirm walk, `civ-flee`, `npc-sit`, mid-`swing`, infected windup and death poses. The two aesthetic corrections are recorded in [the pilot review](../pilot/review.md). Subsequent normal-storage, tiny-triangle and anatomical-sampling fixes keep the same style parameters.

All 337 source/LOD/tier exports pass the unchanged head caps, source heights, topology, UV, texture, skin-weight, inverse-bind and fixed-surface checks. All originally in-range or thicker torso axes meet the ±15% band using fixed baseline anatomical samples. Already thinner axes retain the shrink-only policy. The crawler uses its standing reference profile and retains the approved q260 full head.

The 192 GPU deliveries cover all three LODs of the 38 GPU adult variants, including healthy and infected NPC deliveries used by frozen corpses. Actual game LOD1 lineups and corpse cohorts were captured at both quality tiers. The L1 depot, PO bench and production bench use the same baseline cameras and fixed frames.

Six rendered coffee contacts (man-a, man-b, woman-a at LOD0/1) have **exactly zero distance change** from baseline. Woman-a retains 0.96/0.65 mm clearance; man-a retains its existing 4.16/3.85 mm gap. [Comparison](grips/comparison.json).

Existing angular cloth/atlas seams and partially ground-occluded corpse surfaces remain visible in the untouched baseline. They are documented with paired evidence in the pilot review. The edit introduces no additional visible opened joint seam or detached hand prop in the reviewed pairs. The core export comparison retains baseline auxiliary-eye bounds and legacy rest-cushion findings, with zero new errors; the runtime has zero new LOD fallbacks.
