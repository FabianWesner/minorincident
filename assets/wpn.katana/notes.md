# Katana production model

1.256 m overall, 0.86 m curved blade, 0.33 m two-hand grip, 0.24 m oval guard. Blade points +X; guard touches z=0; footprint centred on X/Y. Required melee attachment empties: root, grip, tip.

Five reviews: initial silhouette and material bands; reduced bevel density; shorter bindings and reference-facing camera; tapered blade-tip bands; wrapped top/bottom grip returns after game-camera review. Static geometry merged into six palette material meshes. The hamon is integrated into the watertight blade mesh, not an overlapping decal. Raised binding ribbons clear grip faces by at least 3 mm. Gold guard has an open centre, recessed dark ring, raised spokes and metal fittings/pommel pins.

No animated components, light emitters, image textures or external brands. Render illumination is studio lighting, not exported. Hero output: 1600×900, 96 Cycles samples. Browser checks cover study front/rear and game views on both backends.
