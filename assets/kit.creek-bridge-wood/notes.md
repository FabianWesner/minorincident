# Creek bridge production notes

Reference measurements interpreted as a 6.2 m long, 2.18 m wide timber footbridge with a roughly 1 m deck elevation and 1 m rail height. The reference includes a rocky planted creek tile; the production kit includes this dressing, with bridge travel along +X and creek flow along Y. Tile bounds: approximately 7.9 × 6 m, ground contact Z=0.

Part list: 26 separately gapped deck planks, paired long bearers, cross joists, ten square newels, ten caps and collars, two horizontal rails per side, iron fasteners, stone pier footings, warm earthen banks, approach paving, faceted blue-green water, raised foam ribbons, rocks, clustered grass and yellow flowering shrubs.

All geometry is static and merged by palette material. No lamps, doors or other animatable parts occur in this reference. Root and deck/railing collider empties are retained. No image textures, no real brands. Grain slivers are raised 4+ mm and water ribbons sit above the highest water vertex. Reduced GLBs are regenerated from LOD0 by deterministic decimation.
