# Courier headlamp

Original procedural asset (repository MIT license). Rebuild headlessly with:

`/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --python assets/acc.headlamp/build.py`

LOD0: 144 triangles, four materials, 13 KB GLB. The +X lens and amber housing sit on a teal cap and charcoal band. `CharacterView` fits it above the female and male couriers' existing cap geometry and attaches it to their `head` node, including the skinned rig. The pickup uses the same GLB. It introduces no light: collection enables L6's existing warm beam and glow.
