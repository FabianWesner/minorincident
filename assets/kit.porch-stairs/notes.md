# Porch and stairs kit

The reference is an exploded modular display: a 2.1 m wide raised landing with three steps, separate 2.8 m wide entry deck with double doors, siding wing, lantern, three potted plants and one loose flowering shrub. Front is +X, ground is Z=0. Cream newels, caps, straight and descending rails; warm wood tread boards and framed door panels; terracotta pots; green foliage with red/cream flowers.

Door leaves are independent multi-material meshes with their origins on the outside hinges. All other surfaces merge by palette material. Trim and raised panels stand at least 3 mm above their backing faces. The glowing lantern includes a light anchor. No image textures or brands.
