# Sunset Grove Freight semi-trailer

Reference proportions: conventional long-nose tractor with tall curved sleeper, twin stacks, five axles, long cream box trailer. Model is 17.2 m long, 2.6 m wide at tyres (3.05 m at mirrors), 4.8 m high at stacks, metres / Blender +X forward / Z up, tyre contact at zero. Trailer is 11.2 m long, 2.5 m wide, with floor at 1.5 m and roof at 4.55 m.

Parts: rounded bonnet and fenders, beveled grille and slats, windshield and wipers, two hinged cab doors with quarterlights and mirrors, sleeper windows and hatch, chrome exhaust/heat shields, fuel tanks and bands, serrated steps, chassis rails, suspension, fifth wheel, ten wheel assemblies with rear dual tyres, riveted trailer rails, vertical panel joints, roof joints, mountain/sun/river emblem, raised orange/navy stripes, lettering, reflectors, landing gear, side and rear underrun protection, mudguards, rear hinged doors with locking rods and hinge hardware, front/brake/clearance lamps, light anchors and cuboid collider empties.

Fictional company changed to SUNSET GROVE FREIGHT CO. Palette colors use registry tokens. Artwork, text, stripes, and reflectors are solid meshes at least 3 mm proud of their supporting surfaces. No image textures. Static geometry merges by material, joint geometry merges by joint/material. All mesh origins are moved to their owning assembly pivot before export.

The manifest still has generic sedan dimensions (4.4 × 1.8 × 1.9 m) for this reference-only asset; this task cannot update it because work is restricted to this asset directory. Integration must update those dimensions to match this GLB.

Measured exported game-space bounds: 17.181 × 4.740 × 3.050 m (X, Y-up, Z). Cab collider width is 2.6 m, including part of the mirror envelope, so the aggregate collision bounds remain within 15% of the visual bounds. Four visual rounds; final counts and backend checks are in report.json / validation.json.
