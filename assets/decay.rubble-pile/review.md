# Visual review

Reviewed production LOD contact and game-reference-peer at 135 and 190 px. Slanted pale slab, broken brick masses and uneven height read as a masonry heap from every side. The near tier has chipped crowns; distant tiers lose small brick fragments but retain the slab, rebar and angular outline. Compared with integrated decay.makeshift-barricade, the material batching and large color areas are consistent; the heap has more angular debris. Reference has much richer chips, timber splitting and surface mottling; those are simplified. Remaining fault: LOD2 is visibly sparse and the largest slab is cleaner than the painted reference. No smooth cone, perforated panels, floating heap, active glow or silhouette collapse seen.

Evidence: `test-results/l5-war-debris-and-craters/decay.rubble-pile/comparison.png`, `lod-contact.png`, `game-reference-peer.jpg`. Headless WebGL2, Metal, shared browser lock, no WebGPU claim.
