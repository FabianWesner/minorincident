# Final review

Four rounds: initial sculpt; broader coat/folds/lean and axe direction; axe grip and garment/face refinements; shield contour and straighter SCBA cylinder.

Reference comparison: red firefighter helmet with ribs and shield; tan coat and trousers; yellow/silver sleeve, hem and ankle bands; dark harness, buckle, gloves and chunky treaded boots; chestnut nape hair; silver rear cylinder with valve, retaining straps and breathing hose; wood-shaft red pick axe. Face includes emissive infected eyes, angry brows, nose/nostrils, cheeks, ears, carved mouth, irregular teeth and tongue. Torso leans slightly forward, knees bend, elbows lead the wrists, and planted soles sit at zero.

The silhouette follows the accepted common-worker's large head and chunky proportions. Height in the Three.js exported bounds is 1.693 m. Every subdivision and bevel is applied; the delivered rigid joint nodes have unit scales and zero rotations. Caps attach to proximal parts and export at zero scale with hidden/stumpFor extras. Pose test rotates armL, foreArmL and legR, toggles stump_armL, and removes armR to expose its shoulder cap visibly. The socketed axe disappears with that hand.

Export validation: 36,948 triangles, 71 GLB meshes (72 Blender mesh objects), all 26 required nodes present, hierarchy valid, palette tokens valid, no textures/skinning. Final capture_glb output verifies both WebGPU and WebGL2 with no warnings or errors. Captures reviewed from opposite sides.

Intentional simplifications: fine fabric distress and helmet badge filigree use chunky palette geometry. The axe has a lower relaxed carry angle than the upscaled reference to align with the gloves. These are cosmetic differences.
