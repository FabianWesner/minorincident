# Infected firefighter

Reference: the red helmet, tan turnout coat, two yellow/silver stripes at sleeve and ankle, black gloves and boots, black SCBA harness with silver cylinder and wooden/red pick axe are retained. The initial emergency-worker sheet confirms the helmet/airpack silhouette.

Adult infected, +X forward, Z up, character right = -Y, feet at z=0. Skull/helmet occupies approximately a third of height, following the accepted common-worker infected. The turnaround stance is relaxed with forward elbows and bent, spread knees; the axe crosses the body.

All sculpt subdivision and bevels are applied. Geometry uses named palette materials and no textures. Each joint is an empty with attached rigid shells; the axe is a child of weaponSocketR. Both weapon sockets and backpackSocket are provided. Stump caps attach to the proximal joint, have hidden=true/stumpFor extras, and export with zero scale for runtime reveal.

Stains ray-project onto their intended outer shell and stand 4 mm proud. Reflective webbing is physical geometry, with silver centers raised 5 mm from yellow backing. Small sculptural overlaps conceal limb seams.
