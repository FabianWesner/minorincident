# Construction worker
Reference studied: upscaled front/side/back/three-quarter turnaround, original crop and infected-workers-and-specials sheet. Accepted inf.common-worker provides rigid-part proportions and applied smoothing patterns. Frame +X forward, -Y right, Z up, feet grounded.

Head approximately one third of crouched height, chunky palms/curled fingers and planted boots. Helmet, short thick chestnut hair, sculpted scream with teeth, glowing red eyes, charcoal torn shirt, orange open vest with raised silver/yellow reflective tapes, dusty torn jeans, loaded leather belt (pouches, hammer, screwdriver, tape measure), padded tan work boots. Each decoration follows its owning rigid joint. Palette read from existing repository tokens; no images. Applied subdivision and reduction before export. Caps remain on proximal joints, export zero-scaled with hidden/stumpFor extras.

The vest uses a saturated safety-fabric variant of `pal_orange`; other materials use existing palette swatches. No palette files were modified. Four review rounds: initial silhouette/outfit; applied reduction, open helmet dome and stronger orange; asymmetric glove/wrap, jagged knee rims and dust; flatter conforming back reflector ribbons. Rigid rest transforms are baked to zero rotations/unit scales so procedural animation can add joint rotations directly. Ground contact is within 1e-8 m of z=0. Approximate finished height 1.65 m including helmet.
