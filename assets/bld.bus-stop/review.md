# Production review

Pass: silhouette, shallow barrel roof and closed end caps, four posts with
stepped feet, framed slogan/house and Sunset Grove transit map, slatted bench,
chunky bevels and fasteners. +X entrance, ground contact z=0. Roof is independently
hideable. Static geometry is joined by palette within body/roof groups. No moving
parts or lamps appear in the reference. Vertex AO is baked at 32 samples.

Five rounds. The fifth fixes a stippled canopy-chip edge by increasing nominal
clearance to 12 mm; curvature therefore cannot reduce separation below 3 mm.
Final WebGPU and WebGL2 study/rear/game captures have no console warnings/errors.
Both game images were visually inspected: poster layers and roof chips are clean.
Hero: 1600x900, 96 samples. Blender game: 960x540, 24 samples.

The shared Vite server developed HTTP 504 Outdated Optimize Dep errors and later
stopped responding. Final checks still used experiment/tools/capture_glb.mjs,
with the unchanged preview/glb.ts bundled by esbuild and served via Playwright
request interception (HTML, CSS, bundle, and exact final GLB). This bypassed dev
HMR/cache failures while preserving the viewer source and both actual GPU
backends. No shared tool or server files were edited. Temporary bundle and
interceptor have been removed. Backend statistics/errors remain in
renders/three-results.jsonl.

No image textures or real brands. Fine photographic surface wear is simplified
to broad palette-coloured chips and wood scars at the Side tier.
