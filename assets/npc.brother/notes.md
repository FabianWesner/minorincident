# Brother hero model
Reference: supplied turnaround and original crop, with living-civilians sheet context. Protected, unharmed child NPC. Relaxed rest stance; +X forward, Z up, right side -Y.
Target height about 1.4 m including cap, head/hair approximately 0.40 m. Separate rigid joint nodes, applied sculpt subdivisions, texture-free palette materials. Identity details: red/cream star cap, brown swept hair, large brown eyes and smile, red zip hoodie with cream drawstrings, charcoal cargo shorts, striped white socks, red lace sneakers, blue star backpack.

Final: 53,892 triangles / 15 rigid GLB meshes, five design rounds plus a corrective re-export within the final round. See review.md and validation.json.
