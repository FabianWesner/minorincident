# Fire axe

Reference proportions: overall length about 1.03 m, head height 0.36 m,
head width 0.23 m, timber section 0.052 × 0.064 m. Six separated dark
rubber wraps cover the last 0.27 m of the subtly curved handle.

The blade points along +X; the cutting edge touches z=0. The reference
is a broad single-bit axe, not a pick-head axe. The poll has an exposed
steel wedge, the lower skirt is unpainted metal, and the heel has a genuine
through-bore. No markings or invented branding are added.

Meshes are joined by material; grip and tip are empty attachment nodes.
No parts of this tool animate separately. Wear geometry stands 3.5 mm
proud of head surfaces. Shared sslib AO bakes 32 deterministic CPU samples
into the exported ao color attribute. No textures, lights, or collision
meshes are exported. The placeholder manifest dimensions are generic;
this build uses plausible hand-tool dimensions from the reference.
