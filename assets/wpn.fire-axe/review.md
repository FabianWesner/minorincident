# Visual review

Five rounds: blockout, wear/grain, asymmetric head, grain simplification,
and a final near-bevel paint-chip adjustment. The failed initial material-slot
build did not produce a review round.

The broad red single-bit silhouette, flared exposed steel cutting edge,
exposed poll wedge, curved warm timber, six separate dark wraps and lanyard
hole match the reference. The tool reads in the game camera. Fine reference
paint/wood scratches are simplified into texture-free palette geometry.
No brands or lettering appear in the reference or model.

Static meshes are joined into six material groups. Grip and tip empties are
retained, and the cutting edge is grounded with +X facing the head.
Details are 3.5 mm proud; no coplanar decals. The final source is limited to
simple profile/sweep helpers, one bore boolean and material joins.

The GLB has 4,566 triangles, six primitives, no image textures, and AO colors.
Final backend results and repeated-camera pixel comparisons are in
backends.jsonl and flicker-check.json. Hero: 1600×900 / 96 Cycles samples.
Game: 960×540 / 24 Cycles samples. WebGPU/WebGL2 study and game captures
are in renders/three-*.png.
