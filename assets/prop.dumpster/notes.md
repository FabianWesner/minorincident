# Dumpster production notes

Reference: wide green steel bin with two dark five-rib lids, side lifting sleeves,
exposed rim, four small offset casters, and warm paint chips; graffiti-free.
Model dimensions approximately 1.368 m depth × 2.630 m width × 1.8725 m height.
Front is +X, lids hinge at -X, all wheel treads touch z=0.
The manifest's 1×1×1 dimensions are placeholder values; no manifest changes are
made by this isolated asset job.

Lids, caster swivels and wheel spins have independent joint empties. Static
parts merge by palette material. Rust chips are closed raised geometry with
4mm face separation. No textures or lettering. Grass palette start colour is
tuned to the reference's muted green steel. AO is deterministic vertex colour.
