# Military cargo truck

Reference proportions: six-wheel conventional cab, overall length ~6.8 m,
width ~2.6 m, canvas crest ~3.6 m. Wheel diameter ~1.24 m; front axle at
+2.12 m, tandem at -1.03 / -2.39 m. Olive steel, khaki canvas, white stars,
fictional A-714 / G-30 / D-3C stencils and Sunset Grove SG 714 hood code.

Direct geometry: scalloped canvas tension bays, arch seams and buckles,
slatted bed and stake rivets, drivetrain/suspension, tread blocks, recessed
wheel dishes and lugs, hinged doors and tailgate, dual windshield/wipers,
mirrors, tanks and straps, steps, grille bars, bumper tow rings and guides.
Markings stand >=6 mm proud. Static geometry joins per palette material;
animated assemblies retain hinge/axle origins. AO is baked on export.

Actual main-model bounds: 6.927 m long × 2.900 m including mirrors × 3.646 m tall.
LOD hardware culling precedes decimation to retain silhouette with ~13% / ~3% density.
