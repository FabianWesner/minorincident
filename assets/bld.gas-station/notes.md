# Sunset Fuel
Reference: cream concrete lot and shop, three fuel pumps (one blue, two red), broad red/blue canopy, two cream columns with yellow guards, flat roof with HVAC, tall sunset emblem/three-price/town sign. Fictional Sunset Fuel / Sunset Grove branding only.

Dimensions: lot 10.3 × 10.4 m; canopy 6.0 × 7.7 m at 4.6 m; shop 3.5 × 7.5 m at 3.5 m; sign 7.7 m high. Front +X, ground z=0, complete lot centered on XY. Raised physical lettering, digital segments and panels; no image textures. Store door hinge at left jamb. Roof and interior are separate visibility assemblies. Light semantics and cuboid collider empties export as extras. Static pieces merge by material within each control assembly. Fixed wall lamps retain named assemblies.

The canonical palette token names are retained; blue and asphalt start values are tuned darker to the reference's navy panels and tar roof. Review rounds: 1 blockout/details; 2 pump/column/sign placement and material/frame refinement; 3 fascia hardware, masonry, cream pump faces, front fill lighting and degenerate cleanup; 4 final framing, compact distant LOD and reusable material merge helper. LOD1 retains controls and full palette at 12% geometry; LOD2 retains root/roof/door controls, omits interior stock and tiny lamp housings, and merges a four-material distant palette at approximately 3% geometry.

The main checkout has no Playwright package. The capture hook resolves an existing e06 worktree installation. The shared server injects a missing `/@vite/client` HMR script; static review browser substitutes an empty JavaScript response for only that development request. All model/renderer requests are unmodified. No shared tools, server files, or references were edited.

Round 5 fixes: canopy trim is a perimeter frame with no broad white surface under the tar; roof seams are real gaps between independent panels. This removed the distant game-camera depth streaks on both backends. LOD2 reconstructs only larger structural components as simple boxes/cylinders, preserves the hero's baked palette-assembly AO, and omits small trim, shelf stock and overlapping paving slabs. Thin distant panels have flat edges; large masses retain bevels. A final export-only LOD optimization follows the five visual review rounds without changing the rendered hero geometry.
