# Clinic annex modeling notes

Source: Level 1 key-locations sheet, region recorded in crop.json. The original crop governs the design; the one-pass imagegen upscale clarifies forms and lettering.

The entrance faces Blender +X, Z is up, all dimensions are metres. Main building footprint is 5.0 × 6.4 m; paving lot 7.6 × 9.6 m; door height 2.25 m; masonry/parapet height 3.85 m; exhaust top about 5.1 m. The reference camera shows the +Y delivery facade. This compact footprint keeps the toy proportions rather than scaling the annex into a hospital.

Parts: cream panelled wall shell, narrow entrance glazing and double doors, teal awning, dark/teal medical name sign with lit cross, wall lantern/card reader, service camera, yellow biohazard panel, deliveries hatch and arrow, bench, yellow entrance bollards and dark side posts, segmented paving/curbs, wooden flower boxes, rear perimeter planting, roof membrane/parapet, HVAC grille/fan, low vents, tall capped exhaust.

Runtime nodes: root/body, roof (all roof equipment), interior (floor/partition/reception desk), door_main and door_main_right (outer hinge pivots), door_delivery (hatch pivot and interaction metadata), window_front/window_side/window_rear and window_door_L/window_door_R (independent emissive assemblies), fx_smoke (exhaust mouth), fx_smoke_window (front glazing), delivery_interaction and front. Light anchors use ss_light, powerGroup clinic-annex and named emissive assemblies; window_front has damaged flicker. No smoke geometry is baked into the pristine reference model.

LOD1 and LOD2 are explicit geometry variants, not destructive wall decimation. Reduced tiers preserve the shell openings, main glazing, sign/cross, hatch/hazard, roof outline/exhaust, bollards and planting silhouette. Flower petals, fasteners, desk fittings, thin weeds and small lettering are removed progressively. Materials come directly from sslib.palette; no textures. AO is baked deterministically on the CPU, clamped to preserve the warm miniature look. Export optimization uses the existing project optimizer with an asset-local definition, without modifying the registry/manifest.

Rebuild: `python3 experiment/tools/blender_run.py ../assets/bld.clinic-annex assets/bld.clinic-annex/build.py -- --glb assets/bld.clinic-annex/model.glb --render assets/bld.clinic-annex/renders/hero.png --width 1600 --height 900 --samples 96 --review-set`.

Validate with `node node_modules/tsx/dist/cli.mjs assets/bld.clinic-annex/validate.ts`; capture compressed GLBs with `sh tools/e2e-lock.sh node assets/bld.clinic-annex/capture.mjs`; assemble and clean review images with `python3 assets/bld.clinic-annex/make_review.py`. The local browser harness uses the same building/world palette swap as AssetRegistry; the study rotates the golden sun toward the reference facade. WebGPU remains manual.

The optimizer invocation is embedded in build.py and imports only shared checked-in project tools. No ignored per-asset helper, reference image or QA script is needed to rebuild the models. contract.json and model*.stats.json are generated by-products. This honors the repository's public asset scope (build.py, model GLBs, notes, review and report).
