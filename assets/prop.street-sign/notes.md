# Street-name sign

Reference: two parallel rounded teal plates, cream perimeter and lettering, one visible hex bolt per plate, a capped round silver post behind the left third, and a dark stepped square plinth. Nominal height 2.87 m; plates 1.16 m wide. The front normal is +X, Blender Z is up, base contact is z=0. Both plates deliberately share a plane and direction, matching the reference rather than inventing a perpendicular intersection sign.

Solid lettering starts 5 mm above the teal face. Teal face overlays protrude 12 mm beyond the cream backing. Washers and hex heads have explicit depth offsets. Rear plates, mounting clamps, and ground anchor bolts complete the unseen side. There are no moving or light-emitting parts. The four static material groups are merged, and the fixed pole includes a non-mesh collider. Palette colors are converted from sRGB to linear before setting Principled BSDF values.

No textures, brands, random geometry, or external fonts. Blender's bundled Bfont is used for reproducible raised text. AO is baked to the vertex color channel with 32 samples and seed zero.
