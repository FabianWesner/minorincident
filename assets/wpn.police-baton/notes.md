# Police baton

0.64 m polymer nightstick, 0.21 m side handle, seven main grip ribs and six side grip ribs. Twenty-sided chamfered profiles match the faceted reference. Black `pal_uiDark` polymer throughout; golden highlights in the reference are lighting, not paint. Closed solid ribbon wrist loop attached through a torus eyelet. Fine woven texture and tiny wear marks are simplified under the no-texture requirement.

All static parts joined into `body`; `root`, `grip` and `tip` preserved. No animatable parts or light anchors. Complete bounds centred in X/Y and lowest vertex grounded at z=0. Deterministic geometry with baked `ao` vertex colours. No decals or coplanar overlays. Script uses only lathe and mesh helpers, with shared preview/export setup inline.

Four rounds: initial shape review; eyelet connection and camera refinement; side-handle outward normals; final bounds centring and high-resolution render. WebGPU and WebGL2 study/game captures match and report no console errors. Grip seams remain readable with no visible z-fighting in game captures.
