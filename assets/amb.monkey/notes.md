# amb.monkey

Derived from the shipped amb.gorilla skin at 55% scale. Continuous weighted limb thinning preserves seams, warm two-tone fur and pale round ears clarify the monkey head, and the hip-skinned tail curls above the rump. All three LODs and low texture variants keep weighted semantic owners. Healthy skins embed idle, quadruped walk/run, grooming and climbing actions in existing crowd clip slots. Rebuild GLBs and production crowd bakes with BASIS=/path/to/basis-encoder-2.1 node --import tsx tools/assets/build-l8-monkeys.ts.
