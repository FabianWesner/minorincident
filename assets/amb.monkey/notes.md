# amb.monkey

Derived from the shipped amb.gorilla skin at 55% scale, with slimmer weighted limbs, a more defined forward-facing head and a curled hip-skinned tail. Keeps the reviewed primate joints and motion clips; healthy/infected atlases preserve the existing eyes and wound distinction. Rebuild with `node --import tsx tools/assets/build-l8-monkeys.ts`. Arena leader is enlarged at runtime. Browser visual QA remains pending.
