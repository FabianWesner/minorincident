# prop.sofa

Reference: old brown three-seat sofa with three upright padded backs, three boxed seats, rolled arms, inset arm ends, short block feet, dark welt seams, and patchy worn leather.

Dimensions: 1.10 m deep, 2.52 m wide, 1.25 m high. Seating front +X; feet grounded at z=0. Static upholstery and feet are merged by six existing palette materials. No animated components or lights are present. Broad wear is tessellated into the original cushion surface; peeled spots and welts are modeled at least 6 mm proud of flat upholstery surfaces; no textures or coplanar decals. Physics is a heavy pushable flammable barricade, with a cuboid collider and 65 kg mass.

The manifest's 1×1×1 dimensions are placeholder dimensions; this build preserves the reference's wide three-seat proportions.
