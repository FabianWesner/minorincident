# Production pickup

Reference: red/ivory retro single-cab truck, open red bed, squared arches,
chunky off-road tyres with perforated steel wheels, three grille bars and
fictional diamond badge. Target dimensions approximately 5.121 × 2.55 × 2.234 m
(including mirrors). Body width 1.74 m; wheelbase 2.85 m; tyre radius 0.49 m.
Front +X, +Z up; final tread contact is shifted to z=0.

Parts: hollow cab and bed, framed glazing, hinged door skins, mirror arms,
roof pressings, bonnet/cowl/wipers, radiator grille, warm headlamp optics,
chrome bumpers, plates, stamped bed ribs/stake pockets, tailgate hinge,
wheel rims/lugs/tread, axles/differentials/springs/frame and mud flaps.

The earlier detailed study supplies measured dimensions and useful mesh helpers.
Production work changes material identities, geometry density, moving assemblies,
static merging, light/physics extras, AO, LODs, arches and finished detailing.
No real brands. Tailgate says SUNSET GROVE; reference registration retained.
All lettering is solid extruded geometry, at least 3 mm outside its plate.

Production export automatically bakes AO and writes model.lod1.glb/model.lod2.glb.
LOD decimation keeps all joint/socket nodes and resets ground contact. The root
collider is 4.8 × 2.2 × 1.9 m; mesh hierarchy is in Blender units, while physics
centerOfMass uses the game’s Y-up convention.
