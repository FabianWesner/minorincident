# Final visual review

Three build/refinement rounds plus the final delivery render, compared against the original crop, upscaled four-view sheet and the emergency-responders concept sheet. Reviewed final front, side, back, three-quarter, pose test, and both browser backends.

Matched identity: camo helmet/front mount/side rails, chestnut nape and sideburns, warm skin and brown eyes, woodland uniform, olive plate carrier with MOLLE/magazine pouches and back US patch, shoulder flag patches, dark gloves/kneepads/thigh holster, tan boots, muzzle-down diagonal rifle and sling. Head with helmet occupies approximately one quarter of 1.429 m total height. Soles at zero; front +X. Raised insignia and panels use separated relief geometry; camouflage uses material regions.

Applied sculpt smoothing and bevels; no exported modifiers, skins or image textures. All required joint and socket nodes present, with verified parent relationships. Pose test rotates armL, foreArmL, legR and shinR: attached sleeve, glove, knee pad, holster and boot follow their joints. Per-joint consolidation simplifies the source and reduces the export from 77 mesh objects to 16 GLB meshes / 90 material primitives, without changing silhouette or triangle count.

Final: 58,443 triangles, under 60k. Hero PNG 1600 × 900 / 96 samples. Turnaround uses four 960 × 540 / 24-sample renders. Pose test uses 960 × 540 / 24 samples. WebGPU and WebGL2 each verified with zero console errors/warnings; whole-character and game-camera companion captures supplied. No reference images or files outside this asset were edited.
