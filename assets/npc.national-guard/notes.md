# National Guard hero

Reference: reference-upscaled.png and original reference.png. Adult toy proportions: approximately 1.43 m helmet height, 0.36 m head-and-helmet region, broad hands and boots. Blender +X forward, +Z up, -Y right; soles touch z=0.

Purposeful shells: woodland field uniform, scalloped ballistic helmet and mount/rails/retention straps, short chestnut hair, olive front/back plate carrier, MOLLE bands and sewn tacks, three magazine pouches, rear US pack panel, radio/aerial, shoulder flags, web belt, thigh holster, segmented gloves, strapped kneepads, bloused trouser cuffs, tan leather boots with tread/eyelets/cross laces, rifle receiver/stock/grip/trigger guard/magazine/vented handguard/front sight/barrel/muzzle and sling.

Geometry uses the existing police-officer loft, rounded-box, hair-lock and studio patterns. Subdivision and bevels are applied before export. Camouflage uses deterministic polygon material regions from existing palette tokens (khaki, apronOlive, khakiSeam, khakiLight), with no textures or coplanar overlays. Static shells consolidate within joints; required animation nodes and sockets stay separate. Rifle is an accessory child of torso. The right forearm gently supports the slung rifle; this pose is baked into geometry and elbow/wrist locations while joint rest rotations remain zero. The left arm stays relaxed for procedural animation.
