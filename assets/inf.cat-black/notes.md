# Infected black cat

Reference: enlarged turnaround and original animal sheet. Low stalking quadruped, arched ragged coat, hooked upright tail, triangular torn ears, red eyes, exposed pink skin islands, open fanged snarl and long whiskers. No clothing or equipment in the reference.

Rest silhouette: about 1.54 m nose-to-tail span; back arch about 0.84 m, ear tip 0.79 m, curled tail about 1.14 m. Broad head about 0.3 m, oversized toes and claws. This is an animal; human head-to-standing-height ratios are not meaningful. Animation uses the infected arm/leg contract, with quadruped leg and paw nodes nested at the same joints. The rigid jaw and tail have independent pivots. Hidden red caps remain attached to the retained joint parent, not the severed mesh.

All sculpt subdivision modifiers are applied to geometry before export as requested. Geometry details merge by joint and palette material. No textures or reference edits.

Orchestrator QA overrides the original infected hero budget for this small crowd animal: LOD0 must be at most 8,000 triangles. Build targets are 7,800 / 3,000 / 1,500 for LOD0/1/2; measured exported counts are 7,471 / 2,842 / 1,343. Widely spaced chunky fur clumps replace dense layered spikes. Joint transforms and cap names are retained across all three LODs.
