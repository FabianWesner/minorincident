# Garden shovel

Reference proportions: approximately 1.18 m overall, 0.30 m blade width, 0.24 m D-grip width. Front/cupped blade faces Blender +X; units are metres. Grounded and centred on its footprint. Wood shaft, red D-frame and wood crossbar, steel fasteners, cupped/dented blade, raised pressed socket, sparse soil clods.

All geometry is static, merged into body with one glTF primitive per palette material. root, grip and tip remain separate named empties. No articulated parts or light anchors apply. No images, branding or coplanar decals. Wood grain is at least 3 mm proud of the shaft, paint/metal wear uses face material assignments, and dirt is volumetric. AO is baked to the ao colour attribute. Build uses a fixed seed.

Four review rounds: initial blockout; camera framing and paint chips; spade shoulder shape, lighter steel and dirt placement; budget simplification, winding and centering. Palette colour patches substitute for texture-based scratches in the reference to retain the chunky Side-tier look.
