# Jogger build notes
Reference: upscaled turnaround, original crop and civilian infected draft sheet. Adult runner with a large chibi head (~30% of total height), forward neck/shoulder hunch and asymmetrical bent legs. +X forward, +Z up, right = -Y. Target height about 1.65 m including loose ponytail.

Purposeful parts: sculpted facial skin; open snarl/teeth/tongue; separate glowing irises; swept bangs, face tendrils and high ponytail; pink visor band and curved brim; ivory short sleeve undershirt, pink sports tank with bound neck/armholes and torn hem; charcoal cuffed shorts, belt, pockets, waist pouch; pink watch; claw fingers; white socks and pink high top sneakers with soles, toe caps, laces, eyelets and side stripes. Blood is raised and conformed to surfaces with >=3 mm clearance.

Rigid animation nodes and sockets follow specs/03 §7. Hidden stump meshes stay as children of surviving parent joints; zero scale plus hidden extras suppress them in GLB, with showScale for activation. Applied subdivision is used per task requirement; no modifiers survive export. Meshes merge by material per joint. No textures.
