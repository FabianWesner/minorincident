# kit.metro-track

Open 12 m track module for the L6 station run (PO 10-15 "Tracks are disconnected"). It is the track of `kit.metro-tunnel` without walls, roof or walkways, with the same numbers: 7.6 m bed, 3.1 m ballast strip, paired rails at +-0.72 m, the same sleeper spacing and rail clamps (clamps are 1 cm taller so they do not share a face with the rail foot). Place it exactly like a tunnel module: y 0.35, centres 12 m apart along X, on the tunnel line (z 15). Rail crown top is 0.62 m above the module origin (0.97 m world), the same as the tunnel's, so the rail endpoints of consecutive modules (and of the last station module and the first tunnel module) coincide. The bed reaches down to the ground (module z -0.35) so no gap shows under it; its collider top (0.2 m, 0.55 m world) is a walkable support like the tunnel's. Metres, glTF Y=0 is the module origin.
