# inf.brute hero construction

Turnaround: 2.2 m infected construction worker, hunch, head about 0.5 m including swept hair; broad trapezius and shoulders; forearms as large as upper arms. Orange open safety vest over torn gray work shirt, crossing yellow/silver reflective tapes on back, belt and square brass buckle, torn cropped gray work trousers, red canvas sneakers with cream toe caps and laces. Hands have four curled fingers and an opposed thumb.

Axes: +X face/front, -Y anatomical right, +Z up, soles at zero. Rigid joint empties own consolidated multimaterial surface meshes. Elbows belong to shoulders, hands to elbows; knees belong to hips and ankles to knees. Applied subdivision on custom sculpt ring meshes, no remaining subdivision modifier. Stumps are child meshes at each contract joint, rest scale zero; `ss_hidden`, `ss_visible_scale` and `ss_stump_for` metadata declare runtime activation.

Canonical palette: infectedSkin, asphalt, woodWarm (orange cloth), schoolBusYellow, sidewalk, uiDark, blood, survivorRed, picketWhite, emi_infectedEye. No image textures. The provided palette has no safety-orange token, so warm orange vest uses woodWarm with yellow reflective borders.
