# Smoke grenade

Reference proportions: canister diameter 0.16 m, painted body height 0.29 m, fuse height 0.09 m. Ground contact z=0; safety lever marks +X forward. Cream identification band and raised curved SMOKE stencil; rolled rims, hollow chimney, hinge and true-hole pull ring. Static geometry joins by material. safetyLever and pullRing have origins at their joints; smokePuff originates at the vent and can be hidden or animated independently.

Smoke uses opaque, smooth-shaded faceted lobes, a texture-free silhouette approximation of the translucent reference. Runtime smoke simulation can hide this separate presentation mesh. The throwable uses the stricter 6k weapon triangle cap, including smoke. No real brands. Stripe is 4 mm proud; lettering is at least 4 mm proud. AO baked to ao color attribute.
