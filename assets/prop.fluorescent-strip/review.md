# prop.fluorescent-strip self review

Reviewed reference, five-sided LOD0 turntable, five matching authored LOD views, and game camera at 135/190 px. This is worker self QA; orchestrator acceptance remains independent.

Long housing, thick end caps and clearly separated twin white tubes remain readable in all tiers. Rear brackets survive. Compared with prop.light-tower, the metal and bright emitter conventions are consistent while this fixture has a clear linear silhouette.

Concrete limitations: Tube highlights clip to white in the golden-hour preview. LOD2 cylinder shading is faceted and socket collars disappear. End caps become square at distance; their thickness survives. The integrated peer is a floodlight assembly, not a second strip.

Initial gate face AO was too dark and fluorescent tubes merged visually; bounded AO/lightened gate paint and a central separator corrected these. Emergency lens was a broad pink highlight; a smaller emissive core and solid red outer lens corrected the red fixture read.
