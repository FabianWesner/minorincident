# Production review

Four rounds: (1) multipart reference blockout; (2) correct framing, softer
normals, reduced bevel density, baked AO; (3) dark studio backdrop and engine
review; (4) tighter biohazard lobes following the engine close-up.

Reference identity preserved: rounded ivory insulated cooler, orange lid and
seals, dark articulated handle, contrasting orange grip, broad warning plate,
front/end latches, reinforced corners and end recess. Wear is simplified to
purposeful raised chips rather than micro-texture detail.

Final game and study captures on WebGPU and WebGL2 load without warnings or
errors. Raised bands, plates and symbol show no visible overlap artifacts.
The exported model has 9,960 triangles, 11 material primitives and five palette
materials. Root/body/lid/handle/collider nodes are present; jointed parts stay
separate. No image textures, brands or external asset dependencies.
