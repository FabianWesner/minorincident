# Production machine gun

Metres, +X muzzle, Z up; visual bounds 1.484 × 0.239 × 0.408 m.
The reference silhouette is reproduced as orange-brown stock/grip, charcoal receiver,
wrapped carry handle, exposed belt, olive box, perforated shroud, long barrel,
rectangular muzzle and single front support. Cooling holes are actual openings.
Materials come from sslib.palette; there are no image textures or brands.

Three visual rounds: (1) full assembly, (2) round shroud and olive magazine frame,
(3) top cooling ports and increased clearance for raised grain. Static meshes join
by material; magazine, trigger and support retain joint origins. Grip/muzzle are
empty sockets. Root physics describes an 8.5 kg pickup; collider is an empty,
not visible geometry. Export bakes 32-sample AO to COLOR_0.

Painted scratches and fine grain from the illustration are simplified to raised
geometric details; colours use the shared palette rather than painted gradients.
Review captures include both backends, opposite study angles and gameplay angle.
