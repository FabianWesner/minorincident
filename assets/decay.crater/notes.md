# decay.crater

Separate small blast crater. Actual angular bowl extends 0.675 m below the grade plane Y=0; torn raised rim and pale ejected stone stand above grade. Dark inner faces make the depression readable. Explicit 24/16/10-sided radial profiles and complete fragments, plus two large torn rim plates in the far tier. No decimation. The fuel crater shares this bowl recipe with its own larger profile and tank remnants.

Terrain interface: terrainCutoutSocket and rimGroundSocket mark grade; blastCenterSocket marks the bottom. terrainCutout.ss_terrain_cutout contains the boundary in local game XZ, depth, and removeGroundCollider=true. col:blastSurface is an EMPTY with collider=trimesh, coordinateSpace=gltf-y-up, flattened vertices and triangle indices sampled directly from the visible bowl. Arrays are asset-local game coordinates; apply the root placement exactly once. Raised debris remains dressing. Do not replace the bowl with an AABB or keep a flat terrain collider across the aperture.

Current runtime blocker: tools/assets/physics-metadata.ts only extracts boxes from size records, and static collision is AABB-based. It does not consume this triangle surface or remove terrain. The crater physics record therefore has boxes=[] intentionally. The level/runtime lane must register a fixed concave Rapier collider and remove the ground inside the authored boundary (or mark the entire crater as an obstacle with a readable detour). This asset lane supplies the exact shape and interfaces; it does not implement district terrain/nav. No emissive material or light anchors.
