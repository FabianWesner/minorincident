# Visual review

Reference and game reviews: 960 × 540, 24 samples. Hero: 1600 × 900, 96 samples.

Silhouette, red/cream canopy, silver cabinet, spoked wheels, handle, bottles and steam chest read clearly. The broad umbrella naturally obscures portions of the counter at the elevated game camera. Trim, bolts, chips and canopy seams stand apart from supporting faces. WebGPU and WebGL2 study/game captures show no visible z-fighting; both backends report no console errors or warnings.

GLB: 11,614 triangles, 23 draw calls, seven materials, no textures; AO COLOR_0 on every primitive. Root/body, four wheel nodes, two hinged door nodes and collider empties exported. Repeated joins simplified into one helper.
