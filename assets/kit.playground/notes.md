# Playground kit

The reference layout is preserved: tower left, red monkey bars behind the slide, two-seat A-frame to the right, and spring duck in front. Frame +X faces the slide exit; all dimensions are metres. The kit is approximately 8.3 × 9.8 m, with a 3.8 m canopy and 1.5 m deck. Palette blue, red and yellow distinguish each element.

Static geometry joins by material. `swing1` and `swing2` pivot at the overhead beam; `springDuck` pivots at the top of its fixed coil. Linked rings form the swing chains. Roof tiles and climbing holds are thick raised geometry, with no image textures or coplanar decals.
